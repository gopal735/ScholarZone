## Conclusion

The design has genuinely been ported into the React application, not merely
approximated. The two documents share the same primary typeface (Inter), the same
display serif with the same weight (Newsreader 400), and the same warm paper
background family, which is the core of the reference's visual identity.

The differences that remain are real and are **not** silent regressions:

1. The hero headline is set at 88px against the reference's 67.68px, and the
   reference's headline copy was replaced. This is the clearest visual divergence.
2. The ink colour is navy in the app and warm near-black in the reference.
3. The app organises the page into 13 sections against the reference's 6, because
   it renders live catalogue sections the mockup hardcodes.

Only two of these are visual-design choices (headline scale/copy, ink hue). I am
not claiming pixel fidelity: I measured computed styles and captured matched
screenshots, but I did not perform a pixel-level image diff, and I cannot certify
the match by eye. Anyone reviewing should open the two PNGs side by side.

## Not a defect, for the record

The reference document has no `id="root"` and no module script. It is a static
mockup with 25 hardcoded sample records. The React app has both and renders 531
real catalogue records. The reference is therefore a design source, not a
functional catalogue, and the app is correct to differ structurally.
