# Verified Scholarships for International Students in Sweden (2026/2027)

> **Excludes the Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships) which is already in your database.**
>
> **Key note for Bangladeshi students:** Bangladesh is eligible for the SI Scholarship Pioneering Women in STEM, and all university-specific scholarships below are open to all non-EU/EEA citizens (including Bangladesh).

---

## 1. Government Scholarships (Swedish Institute)

### SI Scholarship Pioneering Women in STEM
- **Offered by:** Swedish Institute (government agency)
- **URL:** https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/pioneeringwomeninstem/
- **Degree level:** Master's (one- or two-year)
- **Funding type:** Fully funded
- **Eligibility:** Female citizens of Bangladesh, Indonesia, Kenya, Malaysia, Nigeria, Philippines, Rwanda, South Africa, Thailand, or Vietnam; must be liable to pay tuition fees; must have documented work and leadership experience; must apply to a STEM master's programme; must not have previously received an SI scholarship or hold a Swedish degree
- **Application deadline:** Scholarship application portal opens 9 Feb 2026 – closes 25 Feb 2026 (programme application through University Admissions: 15 Oct 2025 – 15 Jan 2026)
- **Coverage/benefits:** Full tuition fee coverage (paid directly to university), SEK 12,000 monthly living allowance, SEK 15,000 travel grant (one-time), insurance against illness and accident, membership in the SI Network for Global Professionals (NFGP)

## 2. University-Specific Scholarships

### Lund University Global Scholarship
- **Offered by:** Lund University
- **URL:** https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/study/admission-degree-studies/scholarships-and-awards/lund-university-global-scholarship
- **Degree level:** Master's (and exceptionally select Bachelor's: Development Studies, Mathematics, Physics and Physical Geography)
- **Funding type:** Partial to fully funded (tuition only)
- **Eligibility:** Citizens of countries outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland who are required to pay tuition fees; must apply to a Lund University programme as first choice on universityadmissions.se; highly selective, merit-based
- **Application deadline:** Usually opens early February 2026; deadline 16 Feb 2026
- **Coverage/benefits:** Grants covering 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% of tuition fees; does not cover living costs

### Uppsala University Global Scholarship
- **Offered by:** Uppsala University (funded by the Swedish Council for Higher Education)
- **URL:** https://www.uu.se/en/study/masters-studies/scholarships/uppsala-university-scholarships
- **Degree level:** Master's
- **Funding type:** Fully funded (tuition only)
- **Eligibility:** Citizens of countries outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland; must apply to a Uppsala University Master's programme as first priority; demonstrate academic talent
- **Application deadline:** Scholarship application period typically 16 January – 2 February 2026 (closed for autumn 2026 intake; next round opens early 2027)
- **Coverage/benefits:** Covers full tuition fee for the Master's programme; does not cover living expenses

### KTH Scholarship

- **URL:** https://www.kth.se/en/studies/master/admissions/scholarships/kth-scholarship-1.72827

## 3. International Joint Degree Programs (Erasmus Mundus)

### Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degrees (EMJMD)
- **Offered by:** European Commission (with participating Swedish universities)
- **URL:** https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters
- **Degree level:** Master's (joint degrees across multiple countries)
- **Funding type:** Fully funded
- **Eligibility:** Open to students from all nationalities/countries; must apply to and be admitted to a specific EMJMD programme involving Swedish universities (e.g., MESPOM with Lund University, GEM with Lund University, EDISS with Mälardalen University, EMPHID with Karolinska Institutet)
- **Application deadline:** Varies by programme (typically January–March 2026)
- **Coverage/benefits:** Full tuition fee waiver, monthly living allowance (~€1,400/month), travel allowance, health insurance, installation allowance, and mobility across 2+ European countries; fully funded with no nationality restrictions

### Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Environmental Sciences, Policy and Management (MESPOM) - Lund University
- **Offered by:** Consortium including Lund University, Central European University, University of Manchester, University of the Aegean
- **URL:** https://mespom.eu/call-applications-2026-2028
- **Degree level:** Master's
- **Funding type:** Fully funded
- **Eligibility:** Open to students from all nationalities; application for EMJMD scholarships separate from self-financing applications
- **Application deadline:** 7 January 2026 (scholarship); 15 April 2026 (self-financing)
- **Coverage/benefits:** Full coverage of participation costs, travel, visa, installation and subsistence costs

### Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Geo-information Science and Earth Observation for Environmental Modelling and Management (GEM) - Lund University
- **Offered by:** Consortium including Lund University, University of Twente, UC Louvain, University of Tartu
- **URL:** https://www.mygemmasters.org/
- **Degree level:** Master's
- **Funding type:** Fully funded
- **Eligibility:** Open to candidates from all over the world
- **Application deadline:** 15 January 2026
- **Coverage/benefits:** Full tuition fee waiver, €1,400/month living stipend, travel allowance (€2,000–3,000/year), health insurance, installation allowance

## Key Recommendations for Bangladeshi Students

1. **Best fully-funded options (tuition + living):**
   - SI Scholarship Pioneering Women in STEM (female students only)
   - IKEA and Södra Scholarship at Linnaeus University (specific programmes only)
   - Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degrees (no nationality restriction)

2. **All university-specific scholarships listed above are open to Bangladeshi students** (as non-EU/EEA citizens), with the exception of KTH India Scholarship (Indian citizens only) and Visby Programme (Baltic Sea region countries only).

3. **Important note about living costs:** Most Swedish scholarships (including SI SISGP and all university tuition-waiver scholarships) cover tuition only, except SI SISGP and Women in STEM which also provide a monthly living allowance. Students must independently demonstrate financial capability for living expenses (~SEK 10,600/month minimum required by Swedish Migration Agency for residence permit purposes).

4. **Application strategy:** Most scholarship applications require first applying for a Master's programme through University Admissions (www.universityadmissions.se) with the target university as first priority. Scholarship applications typically open after admission decisions are announced in March/A April.
