- **Degree levels are wrong on the record.** The catalogue lists "Undergraduate, Master's, PhD", but the official page describes no degree levels. This award is **not a study scholarship**: it funds a peace initiative run by a woman, a formal group, or an informal group. The only tertiary requirement is that one eligible applicant category is "An individual enrolled at a tertiary institution" — with no restriction to any degree level. This unconfirmed claim is the sole reason the verdict is `needs_review` rather than `active`.

General award applications are closed until 2027, so the next re-check should confirm whether the Peace Award runs again in the 2027 cycle.
