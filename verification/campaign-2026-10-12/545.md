## The quotes that carry the verdict

**Awarding body.** The page is the foundation's own and addresses refugees directly:

> "As a refugee in Germany you are entitled under the specific legal requirements to study and to apply for a scholarship – for instance with the Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung."

**Deadline.** Stated exactly as the record carries it, and independently corroborated by the
Promotionsförderung page:

> "Please apply online on campus.kas.de until 15 January or 15 July annually."

> "Jährlicher Bewerbungsschluss ist jeweils der 15. Januar." — *Promotionsförderung* page

At the check time of 12 October 2026 the 15 July 2026 date has already passed, so the
deadline for the cycle that is open now is **15 January 2027**, which matches the record's
`deadline_date: 2027-01-15`.

**Eligibility.** All four claims in the record's `eligibility_summary` appear on the page as
named criteria. The residence-permit claim has two routes:

> "Students with a residence permit in accordance with §24 AufenthG or §61 BAföG can apply at Studienförderung (Bachelors and Masters) as well as Promotionsförderung (PhD)."

> "Students with a residence permit in accordance with §16 AufenthG and §18 AufenthG can apply for our international programme (Masters and PhD)"

> "above average learning or academic achievements at school or university"

> "Values: You identify with the political stances represented by the Konrad Aden Foundation."

> "Volunteering: You are involved in voluntary work in political, social and cultural areas."

> "Initiative: You are willing to actively participate in our scholarship programmm."

**Funding.** A monthly living-cost stipend plus a flat allowance — not tuition, so not
"fully funded" in the tuition sense. The record's `Stipend` wording is accurate:

> "financial support (depending on your income or the income of your parents) up to 752 Euros per month plus 300 Euro for study-related expenses (books, writing material etc.)"

> "financial support of 1350 Euros per month plus 100 Euro for research-related expenses (further allowances possible)"

**Degree levels.** Stated in the same three levels the record carries:

> "The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung supports undergraduate (Bachelor), graduate (Master) and postgraduate (PhD) students."

## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. **The eligibility summary is accurate but incomplete, in a way that costs applicants
   money.** The page requires **German language proof** — "proof of German language skills
   at B2 level" for the study scholarship, and for the PhD scholarship "proof of advanced
   German language skills (at least equivalent to Goethe-Zertifikat C1). A language
   certificate is required for the application." Anyone applying on the strength of the
   catalogue record alone would apply without it. Consider adding it to the summary.
2. **KAS disagrees with itself about the PhD stipend amount.** The refugees page says 1350
   Euros per month; the Promotionsförderung page says "Das Promotionsstipendium wird aus
   Mitteln des Bundesministeriums für Bildung und Forschung gewährt. Es beträgt 1.650 € im
   Monat." The catalogue claims no amount, so the verdict is unaffected — but quote the
   Promotionsförderung figure (it names the funder, BMBF) if an amount is ever published.
3. **The deadline is a recurring pair, not a single date.** The record's `deadline_date` must
   be re-pointed to whichever of 15 January / 15 July comes next at each re-check; today that
   is 2027-01-15.
4. **The recorded URL is a German path with an English/Ukrainian body.** A future re-check
   should not treat the absence of German body text as evidence of a wrong page.
