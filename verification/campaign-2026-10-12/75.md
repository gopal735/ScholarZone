## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **The stored source URL is one cycle stale.** `DocID=73264` is the **2025**-awards terms (£8,000, deadline 24 April 2025, notification by 30 May 2025). The current cycle is the **2027** awards at `https://documents.manchester.ac.uk/display.aspx?DocID=79917` (£12,000, deadline 25 April 2027, notification by 1 June 2027). Every claim in the record matches the 2027 edition, so the record itself is accurate — only the stored URL should be updated.
- **The award is partial, not full funding.** £12,000 as a discount on published overseas tuition fees, first year only for multi-year courses, no cash to the student. It does not cover living costs (the terms even note visa applicants must show they can pay the remainder of tuition and living costs). Do not relabel this record "Fully Funded".
- **Eligibility is by domicile, not nationality.** Predominantly resident in Bangladesh, Pakistan or Sri Lanka for the last three years; self-funded; bachelor's degree taken within South Asia.
- **Country list change worth noting:** the 2025-edition master's terms listed **India** among eligible countries; the 2027 edition lists only Bangladesh, Pakistan and Sri Lanka for the master's award (India remains eligible for the separate South Asia *undergraduate* Global Futures awards). The record correctly reflects the current (2027) list.
- **Cycle churn is annual:** the University republishes the terms PDF each cycle with the year incremented and the amount revised (£8,000 in 2025 → £12,000 in 2027). The next re-check should fetch the then-current edition and confirm the amount and the April deadline date.
