# Record 382 — VU Fellowship Programme (VUFP) — verification memo

**Verdict: active · Confidence: high · Checked 2026-10-12**

## Page read

- https://vu.nl/en/education/more-about/incoming-master-scholarships (official — VU Amsterdam's own "Scholarships for incoming Master's students" page, read in full)

## What the record claimed, and what the page says

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is on vu.nl and presents the VUFP as VU Amsterdam's own scheme: "Our own VU Fellowship Programme (VUFP) is a merit-based tuition fee waiver." It is financially supported by VUvereniging.

**Deadline — confirmed.** The currently advertised cycle matches the record exactly: "Applications are welcome from November 1st 2026." and "Deadline VUFP application: 1 February 2027 (23:59 CET)" (stated twice, in Step 2 and Step 3 of the application procedure). The Master's programme registration that precedes it opened 1 October 2026 (advised by 1 December 2026), so this is the live cycle as of today, targeting the September 2027 intake.

**Eligibility — confirmed.** The page confirms the record's claims:

> "You hold a non- EU/ EEA passport and you are not eligible for the lower tuition fee."

> "Please note that you must have been already conditionally admitted to the Master's programme before you can apply for the VUFP scholarship."

> "When applying for the VUFP scholarship, you automatically apply for the NL Scholarship (formerly HSP) as well. No separate application is necessary."

One nuance: the eligibility bullet list further down says applicants must be "conditionally or unconditionally admitted", while the application-procedure step says already conditionally admitted. The record follows the stricter, procedure-level wording.

**Funding — confirmed.** The record's "merit-based tuition fee waiver + living cost contribution" is accurate as a package: the VUFP itself is tuition-only, and the living contribution comes from the automatic NL Scholarship application.

> "The VUFP scholarship covers the tuition fees only. It does not provide any additional allowance towards study or living costs, like rent, food, insurances."

> "NL Scholarship is a one time contribution towards the costs of living of €5,000 for the entire duration of your study period."

Important caveat: the NL Scholarship is awarded only for the first year, only around 20–25 are available per year, and not every VUFP awardee receives it — so applicants should not count on the living-cost money.

**Degree levels — confirmed.** Master's only, full-time and English-taught, one- or two-year programmes, September start ("students starting in February cannot apply").

## Before trusting the record

- The record's deadline (1 February 2027, 23:59 CET) and application opening (1 November 2026) are current for the 2027 intake.
- Eligibility includes items the record does not list: Dutch-nationality exclusion, prior-degree-obtained-in-Netherlands exclusion, GPA equivalent to Dutch 8, and exclusion of joint programmes VU does not administer.
- The same page's *external* scholarship sections are partly stale (ASML, ECB Scholarship for Women, Jean Monnet still show 2025 dates); this does not affect the VUFP itself but shows the page is not fully maintained everywhere.
