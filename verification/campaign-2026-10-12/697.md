## What the pages say

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The how-to-apply page sits under
"Home / Graduate School" on cshl.edu, footer
"© 2026 Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory. All Rights Reserved." The About
page states: "Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory School of Biological
Sciences is accredited by the New England Commission of Higher Education
(formerly the Commission on Institutions of Higher Education of the New
England Association of Schools and Colleges, Inc.)." This matches the
recorded awarding body, "Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, School of
Biological Sciences".

**Deadline — confirmed for the currently open cycle.** Read today, the
page states: "Applications are now open. Applications must be submitted
online by December 1." The page's FAQ adds: "Applications will be
accepted online during the Fall term (September through December 1).
There is only one admission cycle per year. The School's academic year
begins in August; other start dates are not possible. Without exception,
the deadline for all application materials, including recommendation
letters, is December 1." Applications opened in September 2026 and the
cycle is open today, so the recorded deadline of 1 December 2026 is the
deadline of the currently open cycle.

**Eligibility — all claims confirmed.**

- "The School encourages applicants with backgrounds in any area of
  biology as well as those with non-bioscience backgrounds, such as
  computer science, engineering, math, chemistry, and physics."
- "Students must have received a bachelor's degree or equivalent by the
  time they enroll in the program."
- "Students from non-English language universities must submit TOEFL or
  IELTS test scores."
- "The GRE general test and subject test(s) are not required."
- FAQ, on the record's "no minimum GPA" claim: "There is no minimum GPA
  requirement." (The FAQ also notes there is no minimum GRE or TOEFL
  score requirement, and a $75 application fee.)

**Funding — "Fully Funded" confirmed, not tuition-only.** The page
states: "The Ph.D. program stipend is currently $50,000 per year."
(with $55,000, or the fellowship amount if higher, for students holding
an external NSF/NIH/other fellowship), "The School pays all tuition
costs and associated study fees for its students," plus a laptop and
software/textbooks (~$3,500), a $2,000/year research cost allowance once
in a lab, and "complete health and dental insurance, affordable
on-campus child care, relocation costs, subsidized on-campus dining
costs, and a $350 gym membership allowance."

**Degree levels — PhD confirmed.** The School runs a single doctoral
programme: "Enable students to complete their PhD in an accelerated
timeframe, while maintaining the highest standards of excellence."
About 8–9 students join each year.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The stipend and allowance figures ($50,000/year stipend, $3,500
  materials, $2,000 research allowance, $350 gym allowance) are published
  amounts that can change between cycles — re-quote them at the next
  re-check rather than treating them as permanent.
- There is a $75 application fee, which the record does not mention; it
  is not a contradiction of any recorded claim but is useful context for
  applicants.
- CSHL also runs a separate **BioAI Ph.D. programme** with a different,
  rolling deadline ("Applications will be considered on a rolling basis
  starting December 15 until positions are filled"). That is a distinct
  programme; it must not be merged into this record.
- Next re-check should happen after 1 December 2026, when the 2027 cycle
  page wording ("Applications are now open." / "Applications will open in
  September 2027.") is expected to change.
