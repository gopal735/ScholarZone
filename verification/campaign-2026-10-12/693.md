## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **The 2026 cycle is closed.** The deadline is 6 August 2026 (per the page the
  record cites) — two months past the check date. The record's own
  `deadline_label` already says "2026 cycle - closed", and it should stay
  non-`active` until the next cycle is called. Do not present it as open.
- **The Ministry publishes two pages for this one scholarship with two different
  2026 deadlines** — August 6, 2026 at the URL the record cites, July 3rd, 2026
  at the URL its own Financial Aid index links to. The Government Notice PDF
  that would resolve this is unreadable (SharePoint redirect). A human should
  settle it with the Tertiary Section (tertiary@mes.gov.bb, 535-0863/850-4802).
- **The programme is narrower than the record's eligibility summary suggests.**
  Awards are restricted to three proposed areas of study — Marine Studies,
  Aeronautics, Culinary Arts — and to students of twelve named newer secondary
  schools. Neither restriction appears in `eligibility_summary`.
- **The living allowance is quantified only for the USA, Canada and the United
  Kingdom.** No figure is published for study elsewhere, so funding is not
  uniformly specified across destination countries.
- **Re-check timing.** The ministry called the 2026 cycle on 3 June 2026, so the
  next cycle is likely to be called around May–June 2027. That is the moment to
  look for a fresh deadline, a fresh set of areas of study, and fresh allowance
  figures.
- Everything in this memo was read from the pages themselves; the only source
  that could not be read (the Government Notice PDF) is named above rather than
  inferred from.
