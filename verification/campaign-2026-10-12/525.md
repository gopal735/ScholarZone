## The exact quotes that carry it

**Deadline — the crux.** The record's `deadline_label` is a *verbatim* copy of the page's
"How to apply" sentence, and it says only that applications will open later:

> Applications for this scholarship for the 2027-28 academic session will open soon. In
> the meantime please provide your details and we will be in touch when the scholarship
> is open for applications.

The section ends in a register-your-interest form, not an application form.

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is the University's own, under the breadcrumb
*Scholarships & funding > Sanctuary Scholarships*:

> The University of Glasgow Sanctuary Scholarship has been created to support applicants
> with Refugee or Asylum Seeker status who are currently living in the UK and hold an
> offer to study with us in the 2027/28 academic year.

**Eligibility — confirmed, every distinct claim.**

> Hold a firm conditional or unconditional offer for full time study at the University of
> Glasgow for 2027/26
>
> You must currently be located in the United Kingdom and have Home or RUK fee status.
>
> Have submitted a claim for asylum in the UK **OR** are actively appealing a negative
> decision on claim for asylum in the UK **OR** have been granted Refugee Status,
> Humanitarian Protection (HP) or Discretionary Leave (DL) by the Home Office, as a result
> of an asylum claim.

and, for the resettlement-scheme route:

> Have come to the UK through schemes such as:
>
> - UK Resettlement Scheme (UKRS)
> - Afghan citizens Resettlement Scheme
> - Afghan Relocations and Assistance Policy (ARAP)
> - Syrian Vulnerable Persons Resettlement Scheme (VPRS)
> - Ukraine Sponsorship Scheme (Homes for Ukraine)
> - Ukraine Family Scheme

The "ineligible for mainstream student finance" claim is confirmed too, tied to the full

> The Sanctuary Scholarship also provides a £4,000 per year stipend, to assist with study

> Please note that accommodation costs are not included as part of this scholarship.

**Degree levels — confirmed, with a scope nuance.**

> The scholarship is open to prospective undergraduate and postgraduate taught students at

> This scholarship is not available to PGdip, PDGE or part time programmes.

The page also gives **"Number of Scholarships: 6"** and a long "Eligible programmes" list
spanning undergraduate and taught postgraduate awards.

## What a person should know before trusting this record

1. **The programme is real and correctly sourced, but is not open.** There is no deadline,
   no opening date and no application form — only a register-your-interest form. Anyone
   reading this card on 12 Oct 2026 cannot apply.
2. **The funding type is incomplete.** The record says "Tuition Fee Waiver"; the award also
   carries a stipend. It is neither tuition-only nor fully funded in the
   accommodation-inclusive sense.
3. **The stipend amount is contradictory on the provider's own page.** £4,000 per year
   appears in the body text and the "Value" list, but the two paragraphs for students who
   *do* have access to funding say a £5,000 stipend:
   > Undergraduate students with refugee status (or equivalent) and access to funding, are
   > eligible to apply for the scholarship and would receive the £5,000 stipend towards
   > study costs only, if successful.
4. **"Master's" is narrower than the page's wording.** The page says "postgraduate taught",
   which includes MRes-style awards in its eligible-programmes list, and it expressly
   excludes PGdip, PDGE and part-time study. There is no PhD route.
5. **A typo in the source.** The offer bullet says "2027/26" while the rest of the page says
   2027/28; treat the year as 2027/28.
6. **Extra requirements the record does not mention.** A programme application must be
   submitted to the University *before* the scholarship application
   ("you must have applied to the University before submitting an application for this
   scholarship"), and applications cannot be considered without ARC/BRP (front and back),
   an Immigration Bail Reporting Notice, or a UK share code.
