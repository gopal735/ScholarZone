> "Entrusted by the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (hereinafter referred to as 'UCAS') will recruit outstanding young graduate students from all over the world and provide them full scholarships."

## Eligibility — confirmed

The page confirms the record's summary point for point:

> "Be non-Chinese citizens with a valid passport, friendly to China and abiding by the laws and decrees of China, and UCAS rules and regulations; Be proficient in English or Mandarin; ... Applicants for master's programs must have held a degree or diploma equivalent to a Chinese bachelor's degree and the applicant should be no more than 35 years old. Applicants for PhD programs must have held a degree or diploma equivalent to a Chinese master's degree and the applicant should be no more than 40 years old. ... Applicants must not have accepted any other sponsorship at the time of application and cannot accept any other sponsorship during the period of this scholarship. Applicants should study full time in the research institute he/she is enrolled in and are not allowed to take any other job during his/her studies."

## Funding — confirmed as fully funded

> "Successful PhD students who are awarded a scholarship are exempted from the tuition fee of RMB 40,000/year. ... Successful Master students who are awarded a scholarship are exempted from the tuition fee of RMB 30,000/year. ... Successful PhD applicants will receive a monthly stipend of RMB 5,000 ... [Accommodation] Successful applicants could live in UCAS Beijing campus for free or apply for an accommodation subsidy for those who live off Campus ... [Medical Insurance] RMB 800/person."

Tuition and application-fee exemption, monthly stipend, housing or subsidy, and medical insurance: a genuinely fully funded award, exactly as the record's `Fully Funded` claims.

## Degree levels — confirmed

> "Sponsorship Period Master students: 36 months (with no extension) PhD students: 36 months (with permitted extension of no more than 12 months)"

Master's and PhD only — matching the record's "Master's, PhD".
