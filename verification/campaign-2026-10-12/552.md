### Eligibility
The record's core claims are confirmed: candidate must be a citizen or permanent resident of the listed North American and European countries and must either hold a doctorate obtained within six years as of April 1 of the fellowship's fiscal year, or be enrolled in a doctoral course expecting a PhD within two years of the fellowship start.

> "(*) Eligible countries are the US, Canada, European Union countries, UK, Switzerland, Norway and Russia."
>
> "have obtained a doctoral degree from a foreign graduate school and, As of April 1 of the fiscal year, are within six years of having obtained that degree (i.e., obtained their degree on or after April 2, six years prior to the fiscal year), or who are currently enrolled in a doctoral course at a foreign graduate school (equivalent to the doctoral level) and are expected to receive a Ph.D. within two years from the start date of the fellowship period."

Minor nuances (not material): the page says "citizen or permanent resident" rather than the record's word "resident"; Russia remains on the eligible-country list; researchers with 3+ years continuous research in an eligible country are also eligible.

### Funding
The record's "Stipend" funding type is accurate. The award is a maintenance allowance plus travel and insurance — a funded research stay, not tuition support:

> "(1) Airfare: A round-trip air ticket ... (2) Maintenance Allowance: (a) For PhD holders: JPY362,000 per month (b) For non-PhD holders at the time research starts in Japan: JPY200,000 per month (3) Miscellaneous: A settling-in allowance of JPY200,000 (only applicable for Fellows with 3 or more months of tenure), Overseas travel insurance, etc."

### Degree levels
The record's "PhD, Postdoctoral" matches:

> "The program provides PhD students or PhD researchers in the Europe and North America with opportunities to conduct collaborative research under the guidance of their hosts in universities and other Japanese institutions for a relatively short period of time."

- Fellowships run 1–12 months, in all fields of the humanities, social sciences and natural sciences.
