**Deadline — confirmed.** The programme page shows "Intake Status: Open" and "Due date: Continuous" for the "Graduate Student Scholarships Program - Seasonal Intake," and states the intake open date is university-specific, exactly as the record says. **Eligibility — confirmed.** The programme page: "This scholarship program is open to Canadian, permanent resident, and international graduate students conducting full-time research in key emerging technology areas of information and communications technologies (ICT) and advanced materials and manufacturing technologies (AMM) at one of the following post-secondary institutions" — followed by the five named institutions (Athabasca University, Concordia University of Edmonton, University of Alberta, University of Calgary, University of Lethbridge). The record's "develops rather than merely uses" and "impact for Alberta" claims are on the UCalgary page: "Research projects involving the development of a technology that is not an ET but utilizes ET as a tool, it would be categorized as the use of ET, rather than ET development. **Funding — confirmed as Stipend.** The programme page: "The program offers funding ranging from $12,000 to $31,000 per year, and the terms vary from one to four years, depending on the recipient's time in the program." UCalgary: "$26,000 master's, up to two years / $31,000 doctoral, up to four years / $18,000 AGES top-up value / $12,000 Major Award top-up value". A stipend-type scholarship, neither fully funded nor tuition-only — matches the record.

**Degree levels — confirmed.** Master's and PhD. UCalgary: "Open to thesis-based students undertaking a full-time graduate program in research areas that are strategically important to Alberta," with level-specific award values.
