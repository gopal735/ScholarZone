## The quotes that carry the verdict

**Deadline — currently open**

> "El plazo de postulación se inicia el 1º de agosto y cierra el 30 de octubre del 2026, a las 23:59 horas."

Opened 1 August 2026, closes **30 October 2026 at 23:59**. Today is 12 October 2026, so the cycle is open. This matches the record's `deadline_date` of `2026-10-30` exactly.

**Eligibility — as recorded**

> "Las Becas están dirigidas a Oficiales, Suboficiales o Tropa de las Policías Uniformadas de los países interesados en incorporarse al Programa. No se aceptarán postulantes de instituciones policiales civiles. Los postulantes no hispanoparlantes, deberán poseer un dominio medio/medio alto del idioma castellano."

And on the mandatory documents:

> "DOCUMENTOS OBLIGATORIOS — Pasaporte Oficial (Obligatorio) — Seguro de Vida (Obligatorio)"

Officers, NCOs and enlisted personnel of uniformed police forces only; no civil police institutions; medium/medium-high Spanish for non-native speakers; official passport and life insurance compulsory. All four claims in the record confirmed.

**Funding — partially funded, correctly classified**

> "Cada Beca otorga subvención para los siguientes ítems: Gastos de arriendo y alimentación (externado); Gastos de alojamiento y alimentación (internado); Cobertura médica y dental primaria (sólo ante circunstancias derivadas de actividades propias del curso); Vestuario y equipo necesario (solo cursos de formación)."

In-country living costs and course-related insurance, clothing and equipment only. **No airfare, no tuition/enrolment payment, no stipend** is mentioned anywhere on the page — so "Partially Funded" is right and the record must never be presented as "Fully Funded".

**Degree levels — "Other" is correct**

> "Las Becas ofrecidas por el Programa de Cooperación Internacional del Gobierno de Chile para Policías Uniformadas Extranjeras CECIPU, se otorgan para realizar estudios de Formación, Perfeccionamiento y Especialización Policial, en planteles docentes de las Fuerzas de Orden y Seguridad de Chile (Carabineros de Chile y de la Policía de Investigaciones de Chile), conforme al ofrecimiento que se hace anualmente."

The courses offered run 2 to 8 semesters (e.g. an 8-semester officer formation course, 4-semester "Oficial Graduado en Ciencias Policiales", 2-semester specialities). This is police training, not academic Bachelor's/Master's/PhD study.

**Awarding body**

The programme is run from its own government site, `cecipu.gob.cl`. The official 2027 application information sheet gives the contact addresses `ssp-cecipu@minsegpublica.gob.cl` and `cmunozv@minsegpublica.gob.cl`, i.e. Chile's Ministry of Public Security, and the page repeatedly refers to "La Secretaría Ejecutiva del Programa CECIPU". The dated DIGEMPOL page adds that the programme is "administrado por una Comisión Ejecutiva Interministerial" and was created by Decree No. 600 of 18 October 1995.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. **The window closes in 18 days.** Applications for the 2027 intake close 30 October 2026 at 23:59 (Chile time). After that date this record is no longer an open opportunity until the next cycle is published.
2. **Travel is not covered.** The scholarship does not pay for international airfare or a tuition fee. Anyone budgeting from this record should assume they fund their own flight.
3. **You cannot apply directly.** Applications go through the applicant's own police institution — the site publishes a "Carta Autorización" (authorisation letter) and a "Carta Invitación" (invitation letter) for the institution's head to complete. It also cannot cover family members.
4. **Two pages on the same topic, only one current.** `cecipu.gob.cl/becas-de-estudios` shows the 2026 cycle. The mirrored DIGEMPOL page at `digempol.interior.gob.cl/cecipu` still shows the 2022 deadline and a November results month — it is a stale copy and must not be used to date the record. The CECIPU page itself reports results in "la segunda quincena del mes de diciembre".
5. **Benefit wording drifts slightly.** The older DIGEMPOL text also mentions "Práctica policial", "Vestuario Deportivo (curso internado)" and vaccination validation. Re-read the benefit list on `cecipu.gob.cl` at the next re-check rather than copying this memo forward.
6. **Re-check timing.** The page rolls to the next year around early August; the natural next verification point is shortly after 1 August 2027, or immediately if the 30 October 2026 date passes without a new date being posted.
