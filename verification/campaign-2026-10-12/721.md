## Why not `active`

The record is accurate, but the only published cycle is closed. The Ministry page states: **"Applications for 2026 tertiary scholarships are now closed"**, and Le Va states: **"Applications for the 2026 Toloa Scholarships are now closed."** As of 12 October 2026 no 2027 round dates are published (the 2026 round opened 18 November 2025 for the 2026 academic year). With no currently-open cycle, the record cannot be verified as open.

## Exact quotes carrying this verdict

- "The Toloa Scholarships support Pacific students to pursue study in Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM). Scholarships are available at both secondary and tertiary levels." (MPP)
- "Tertiary scholarship applications closed at 5pm on Monday 9 February 2026." (MPP)
- "Scholarships for tertiary students in 2026 are available to those who have completed at least one year of study and are continuing full-time STEM study. Students entering their second year or beyond may apply." (MPP, repeated verbatim by Le Va)
- "Tertiary students in STEM courses can receive up to $10,000 for one year of full-time study. This may cover tuition, compulsory course related costs, accommodation expenses for university halls and, for PhD students, research or fieldwork expenses." (Le Va)
- "The scholarship is only open to New Zealand citizens or permanent residents of Pacific Islands descent." (Le Va)

## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. The catalogue entry is honest and correct as of the last cycle — it is simply not currently actionable. Expect the next (2027) round to open around November 2026; re-check then before marking the record active.
2. Only 10 tertiary scholarships are offered per year (400 secondary), so stating the competitive scale on the card would help applicants.
3. Two record fields would benefit from refinement: `funding_type` "Tuition Fee Waiver" understates what the up-to-$10,000 award can cover, and `eligibility_summary` omits the NZ citizenship/permanent-residency + Pacific-descent requirement and the exclusion of students eligible for the government final-year fees-free scheme.
