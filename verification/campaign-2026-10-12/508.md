# Flanders Trainee Programme — record 508

**Verdict: conflicting (high confidence).** Checked 2026-10-12 against the Government of Flanders' own pages.

## What the official pages say

- **Awarding body**: Government of Flanders, Department of Foreign Affairs — confirmed official.
- **Deadline**: this is the material problem. The record publishes "Closing date for applications: 1st March" with deadline date 2027-03-01. The official application page states:

  > "You can submit your grant application up to six months in advance until one day before the start of your internship."
  > "In principle, you can apply for a grant at any time of the year, but you can only get a grant if there is still budget available."

  There is no 1 March closing date. Applications are rolling and budget-limited.
- **Availability**: the main page states "No more grants for 2026 ... There is no more budget available for 2026. A budget will be available again in 2027. You can reapply for internships taking place in 2027 as from January 2027." So the programme is not currently open.
- **Eligibility**: matches the record (under 35 at internship start; a diploma obtained at a Flemish educational institution; an internship of at least two months with an organisation on the published list).
- **Funding**: the record says "Stipend". A "Grant amounts" sub-page exists but the figures are not on the pages read, so the amount is unconfirmed.

## Before trusting this record

The deadline model must be replaced (no closing date, budget-limited). Whether an exhausted 2026 budget means the record should be closed/archived for this round is a reviewer decision — the programme returns in January 2027.

Sources: vlaanderen.be programme page and its application-and-procedure page.
