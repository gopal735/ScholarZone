  > "Applicants who already hold a PhD at the time of application are eligible to apply only if they obtained it during the two years prior to the date the application is submitted."
  > "Applicants must have at least one first author publication (research paper) accepted in or published in an international peer reviewed journal at the time of application. - **Funding** — confirmed as "Stipend and Salary". In EMBC Member States EMBO funds the host institution to issue an employment contract; fellows hosted elsewhere receive a stipend. Relocation/travel allowance and child daycare/dependent allowances included. The page is explicit that bench fees, overheads and consumables are **not** covered.
  > "EMBO provides grants for host institutions in EMBC Member States to issue employment contracts to EMBO Fellows. Fellows working in other countries than EMBC Member States will receive a stipend."
- **Degree levels** — confirmed as Postdoctoral. Doctorate required at fellowship start; duration up to two years, minimum one.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The scheme is rolling: applications are accepted throughout the year, with cutoffs (currently 22 January 2027, 14:00 CET). Dates after that will move to the next cutoff, so re-check the banner before each cycle.
- Two rule changes are posted and worth knowing:
  - From the Autumn 2027 selection round (applications after 22 January 2027, 14:00 CET), re-applications will no longer be permitted — one EMBO Fellowship Proposal per researcher.
  - From the Autumn 2026 round, a host laboratory may support a maximum of one candidate per selection round.
- What fellows actually receive differs by host country (salary via employment contract inside EMBC Member States vs stipend elsewhere), so applicants should read the country-specific contract/stipend rate sheets linked from the benefits page.
