## What was confirmed

**Awarding body.** The page is on searca.org and names the organisation in full:

> "Southeast Asian Regional Center for Graduate Study and Research in Agriculture (SEARCA), UPLB Campus, College, Los Baños, 4031 Laguna, Philippines"

The footer adds that SEARCA is "one of the oldest among 26 specialist institutions of the Southeast Asian Ministers of Education Organization (SEAMEO)". This is the awarding body's own site, not an aggregator or news article.

**Deadline.** The dedicated programme page shows no deadline. The parent scholarships page publishes the application timeline:

> "Submission to the Ministry of (Higher) Education: 1 September–31 October 2026"

and

> "SEARCA will release the scholarship application results starting 31 May 2027 only for those with university acceptance letters."

That window is **open today** (12 October 2026), and the 31 October 2026 date matches the record. Note the route: applicants first submit to their own Ministry of (Higher) Education; only shortlisted applicants then upload via the SEARCA iSCHO Portal (1–31 December 2026).

**Funding.** The programme page states:

> "The SEARCA Full Master's and PhD Scholarship Program is fully funded by SEARCA to support highly qualified individuals from SEAMEO member countries."

Entitlements listed: tuition and other school fees; travel allowance (roundtrip air ticket from home country to study post and local transportation); thesis/dissertation support; book and supplies allowance; health insurance; stipend for food, lodging and incidental expenses. A full package — consistent with the record's "Fully Funded" (not tuition-only).

**Degree levels.** Explicitly the Full Master's and PhD Scholarship; the application procedure distinguishes "Applicants for MS degree" from "PhD degree applicants". Matches the record's "Master's, PhD".

**Study posts (non-joint programme):** Institut Pertanian Bogor (IPB), Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM), Universitas Brawijaya (UB) — Indonesia; Kasetsart University — Thailand; Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) — Malaysia; University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB) — Philippines.

## What a reviewer should know before trusting the record

- The record's `eligibility_summary` is **empty**. The live page shows the award is materially narrow: "Open to nationals of SEAMEO member countries who are regular employees of academic or research institutions or government agencies" and "Must not be more than 40 (Master's Degree) or 45 (PhD) years of age at the time of application". It is not a study-anything-anywhere-anyone award — only SEAMEO member-country nationals already employed in academia, research or government. A human should decide whether to add this to the record.
- The deadline does not appear on the programme's own page; it is in the application timeline on the parent `/scholarships` page. Future re-checks should open both.
- Applications are not sent directly to SEARCA at the first stage — they go through the applicant's Ministry of (Higher) Education, and only endorsed applicants get portal access.

## Verdict rationale

Official pages on the awarding body's own domain were read directly; awarding body, deadline, funding and degree levels all agree with the record, and the deadline belongs to the currently-open cycle (window 1 September–31 October 2026). No record claim was contradicted. `active` with `high` confidence.
