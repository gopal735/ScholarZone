## Eligibility — confirmed

The record's summary matches the page:

> "This is a scholarship program for privately-financed international students with excellent academic and character records who are enrolled in undergraduate schools, graduate schools, junior colleges, colleges of technology, specialized course in specialized training colleges, university preparatory courses or Japanese language institutes in Japan ... and are facing financial difficulties."

Income claim matches, with a nuance:

> "If the candidate has a financial supporter in Japan, his/her annual income must be less than 5 million yen."

The record's "supporter's annual income <5 million yen" is correct; the page makes this condition apply specifically when the candidate has a financial supporter *in Japan*. The record's phrase "about to enroll" is not on this page — it corresponds to the separate Reservation Program pages in the same programme section.

## Funding — confirmed as partial

> "Graduate/undergraduate level : 48,000 yen per month"
> "Japanese language institutes : 30,000 yen per month"
> "12 months (From April to next March) or 6 months (From October to next March)"

A monthly stipend only — no tuition or enrolment coverage — so "Partial" in the record is correct, and the award is definitely not fully funded.

## Degree levels — confirmed (record is a subset of what the page lists)

> "The candidate is enrolled as a full-time student in an undergraduate school, junior college, college of technology (3rd year or above) or specialized course in a specialized training college in Japan."

> "The candidate is enrolled in a Japanese language institute with the objective to advance to an undergraduate school, graduate school, junior college, college of technology or specialized course in a specialized training college in Japan."

Every level in the record (Undergraduate, Graduate, Japanese Language Institute, College of Technology, Specialized Training College) appears on the page. The record's list omits junior colleges and university preparatory courses, which the page also covers; this is an omission, not a contradiction.
