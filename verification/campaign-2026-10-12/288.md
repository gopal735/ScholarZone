## What a person should know before trusting this record

Do not treat this as an open BME scholarship. The record appears to be a mis-attributed **Stipendium Hungaricum** entry: a Hungarian Government programme managed by Tempus Public Foundation, for which BME is one of ~30 host institutions. Every verifiable field in the record (deadline, funding, degree levels) describes Stipendium Hungaricum, not a BME award. The decision a human must make: re-attribute the record to Stipendium Hungaricum (awarding body: Hungarian Government / Tempus Public Foundation) or quarantine it. If kept in any form, its canonical URL is bme.hu/en/scolarships (misspelling is BME's own), and re-check for the 2027/28 call around November 2026 — historically the call opens in November with a mid-January deadline.
