## Eligibility — confirmed

- Degree claim: "You hold a PhD by thesis or a degree or a diploma or certificate recognized as equivalent in accordance with European Union directives or a bilateral agreement."
- Recency claim: "You can apply for a junior postdoctoral fellowship if your PhD was obtained no longer than 3 years before October, 1 of the year in which the fellowship would start, or will be obtained at September 15 of that year at the latest"
- Supervisor claim: "You have to find a supervisor at a Flemish host institution (article 3 of the regulations on postdoctoral researchers)."
- No repeat claim: "You have not yet benefited from this fellowship, not even partially."

Additional condition not in the record: "You can apply maximum two times for the same fellowship." (an added restriction, not a contradiction).
