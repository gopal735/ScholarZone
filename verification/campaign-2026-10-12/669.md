## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. **Degree levels are imprecise.** The catalogue lists "PhD", but this is a 3-month research/advanced-training fellowship that awards no degree and explicitly has a graduate track (minimum M.S., age ≤35) alongside the post-graduate track (minimum Ph.D., age ≤45). Suggested value: "M.S./Ph.D. (research training fellowship; no degree awarded)".
2. **Funding is genuinely partial.** COMSTECH pays only international low-cost airfare; subsistence and supervision are expected from the host institution. "Partially Funded" is correct; do not read it as fully funded.
3. **The call is open with no closing date.** Applications go by email/online form to the COMSTECH Secretariat in Islamabad; there is no published deadline, so the record's rolling label is accurate but the call could close at any time.
4. **Eligibility confirmed as written:** OIC member-state citizens employed by a research institution in an OIC country, in any area of biomedical sciences.
5. Minor: the record's region "Middle East" understates the reach — eligible countries are all OIC member states ("Country: Multiple" is correct).
