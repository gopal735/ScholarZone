## Why `needs_review`, not `active` or `conflicting`

The only mismatch is the `degree_levels` field. The record labels the fellowship "PhD", but the official page explicitly opens it to M.S. holders (graduate-studies track) as well as Ph.D. holders, and the fellowship confers **no degree** (it is a 6–12 month research/advanced-training stay). That means one claim cannot be confirmed as written, which is exactly the `needs_review` condition.

It is **not** `conflicting`: the deadline, funding, award and awarding body all match — there is no material contradiction in any of those categories. The degree-level label is a classification gap, so a failure to confirm it is the correct outcome.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The official page is live and self-consistent; the programme exists and the call is **currently open** (rolling, no fixed date, so it is verifiable as open).
- Funding is genuinely **partial**: COMSTECH pays only the low-cost international airfare, and lodging/subsistence/supervision are only "expected" from the host institution. There is no stipend and no tuition. Do not present this as fully funded.
- **Fix the `degree_levels` field.** It currently reads "PhD" but the scheme is open to holders of M.S. or Ph.D. (and grants no degree). Suggested value: something like "Master's / PhD researchers (research fellowship; no degree conferred)". Until that is corrected, keep the record at `needs_review`.
