## Eligibility — confirmed

> "To be eligible for a Gilman scholarship, an applicant must be: A citizen or
> national of the United States; An undergraduate student in good standing at an
> accredited institution of higher education in the United States (including
> both two-year and four-year institutions); Receiving a Federal Pell Grant
> during the time of application or provide proof that they will be receiving a
> Pell Grant during the term of their study abroad program or internship; In the
> process of applying to, or accepted to, a credit-bearing study abroad or
> internship program."

Confirms every claim in the record's eligibility summary: American nationals,
undergraduate level, high financial need (the Program Overview uses "high
financial need" verbatim), Federal Pell Grant, and a credit-bearing study
abroad/internship programme.

## Funding — confirmed as "Partially Funded"

> "The U.S. Department of State's Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship
> Program (Gilman Program) provides up to $5,000 in merit-based scholarships to
> outstanding American undergraduate students with high financial need to
> pursue credit-bearing academic studies and career-oriented internships abroad."

> "Award recipients are chosen by a competitive selection process and must use
> the award to defray eligible study or intern abroad costs. These costs include
> program tuition, room and board, books, local transportation, insurance,
> international airfare, passport and visa fees."

A capped award of up to $5,000 that defrays listed costs is partial funding,
not fully funded — the record is correct. Limited supplemental STEM and Critical
Need Language Awards add funding on top of the standard award.

## Degree levels — confirmed as "Undergraduate"

> "Nearly 3,000 scholarships of up to $5,000 will be awarded this academic year
> for U.S. citizen undergraduates to study or intern abroad."

Undergraduate only; no graduate level.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **Timing:** As of 12 October 2026 the programme is *between cycles* — the
  Fall 2026 application closed 1 October 2026 (advisor certification closed
  8 October 2026; notifications due December 2026) and the Spring 2027
  application opens mid-January 2027, with the next student deadline
  **25 February 2027, 11:59 pm Pacific Time**. The record's deadline label
  already states this accurately, so the card does not mislead.
- **Deadline time zone is Pacific Time and late applications are not accepted**
  — worth showing to applicants outside the U.S.
- The eligibility page adds conditions the record's summary does not mention:
  the destination must carry a U.S. State Department Travel Advisory Level 1 or
  2, and programmes cannot involve institutions on the NDAA Section 1286 list
  (relevant for China).
- A new **Gilman Skills for America Scholarship** (up to $5,000, two-year
  college students, no additional essay) now appears on the Program Overview
  page; it is still undergraduate level, so the degree-levels field stays
  correct.
- Cosmetic only: the site's countdown banner renders as unrendered template
  text on every page ("Application for GILMAN and GILMAN-MCCAIN ends in {day}
  Days : {hour} Hours"). This does not affect any record field.
