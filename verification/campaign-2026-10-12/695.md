## Claims adjudicated (all snippet evidence from fulbright.ca itself)

- **Awarding body — confirmed.** The official instruction PDF on fulbright.ca:
  > "The Killam Fellowships Program, administered by Fulbright Canada, offers
  > exceptional undergraduate students from the United States the opportunity
  > to spend one semester or a full academic year studying at a university in
  > Canada."

- **Deadline — confirmed.** From
  `fulbright.ca/programs/killam-fellowships/canadians/application-deadlines`:
  > "The deadline for the open competition for the 2027-28 Killam Fellowships
  > competition is January 31, 2027."

  This matches the record exactly (`31 January 2027` / `2027-01-31`) and is
  the currently-open cycle as of 12 October 2026.

- **Eligibility — confirmed.** From the main programme page:
  > "The Killam Fellowships Program provides an opportunity for exceptional
  > undergraduate students from universities in Canada and the United States
  > to spend either one semester or a full academic year as an exchange
  > student in the other country."

  And from the Canadian eligibility page: applicants must "Be Canadian
  citizens" and "Be full-time undergraduate students in good standing at a
  degree-granting institution in Canada". Minor nuance: the official wording
  is "from universities in Canada and the United States"; the record's
  "participating ... universities" phrasing applies specifically to the
  direct-exchange (partner-institution) route, which the record also
  describes.

- **Funding — confirmed as a stipend, not fully funded.**
  > "The Killam Fellowships Program offers a cash award of $6,000 US for a
  > one semester exchange ($12,000 US for the academic year), along with a
  > health benefit plan."

  Tuition is not covered (direct-exchange fellows keep paying their home
  institution), but the record only claims "Stipend", so there is no
  contradiction.

- **Degree levels — confirmed.** Undergraduate (Bachelor's) only, matching
  the record.
