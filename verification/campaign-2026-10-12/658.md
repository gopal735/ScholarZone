## What a person should know before trusting this record

- The award is **partial funding**: NZ$15,000 off tuition, one year. It does not
  cover flights, living costs, insurance, or the remainder of tuition.
- Application is automatic via "Apply to study"; there is nothing to submit by a
  deadline, and the decision appears on the admission offer.
- Eligibility is limited to the fifteen named degrees (e.g. Bachelor of Nursing,
  Bachelor of Engineering-type agribusiness/science programmes); other
  undergraduate degrees are directed to Massey's separate Toroa International
  Undergraduate Fee Scholarship.
