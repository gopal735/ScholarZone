## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The record was accurate on every verified field at the time of this check; no field updates
  are recommended beyond restating the deadline wording.
- **The deadline is 3 days away (15 October 2026, midnight Paris time).** After that date the
  Cycle 2026 application route closes and the record must be re-checked against the next
  cycle before it can stay `active`.
- Eligibility is nationality-restricted: the 2026 cycle invites candidates from Africa, one
  Arab State (Bahrain), Asia-Pacific SIDS, Latin America and the Caribbean, plus Türkiye and
  Ukraine (Annex 1). Applicants elsewhere are not eligible regardless of other merits.
- Applicants cannot apply directly: only applications nominated by a UNESCO National
  Commission are considered, and candidates must reach their Commission at least 2 weeks
  before the deadline.
- The award is a lump-sum grant of up to US$10,000 for a 3–9 month research stay. It is a
  stipend-type award, not a fully funded scholarship — tuition, equipment and publication
  costs are excluded. Anyone presenting this record as "fully funded" would be overstating it.
