Affairs, with its New Taipei City address in the footer. The award appears under
"Fu Jen Scholarships", i.e. the university's own institutional award.

**Deadline — confirmed, and currently open.** The Key Dates page publishes both

> 第一梯次 The first round … 2026年10月15日13:00至12月4日23:59
> October 15 (13:00) to December 4 (23:59), 2026

for Fall 2027 (second round: "March 1 (13:00) to May 15 (23:59), 2027"). The Spring
2027 window is open right now — it closes 14 October 2026, two days after this check —
and Fall 2027 opens the next day. `deadline_date` 2026-10-14 is correct.

**Eligibility — confirmed.** The page states the award is applied for together with
the admission application and that admission is a precondition:

> "Please apply for the scholarship when applying for admission on the online system.
> For the latest information, please refer to the admission brochure for that

> admission and after the recipient has completed registration."

The record's "tick the scholarship when submitting the online admission application"
is a fair reading of this, but it is a paraphrase — the page does not describe a
literal checkbox.

**Funding — confirmed.** Tuition/miscellaneous fee waiver or reduction plus a
stipend, for eight semesters, matching the record's "Tuition Fee Waiver, Stipend":

> "The scholarship is awarded for eight consecutive semesters. The scholarship begins
> in the semester for which the recipient was granted admission and after the recipient
> has completed registration. Tuition and miscellaneous fees are waived or reduced as
> part of the scholarship and the recipient receives a stipend of NT$50,000 per
> semester. The stipend is disbursed in monthly installments of NT$10,000 after the
> recipient completes registration for the semester."

**Degree levels — confirmed.** The page has a dedicated section:

> "New International Students in Doctoral Degree Programs"

Undergraduate and master's students are covered by different awards on the same page
(four semesters at NT$10,000/30,000/50,000 per semester for those enrolled AY 114 and
before; a single semester-of-admission NT$10,000 for AY 115), so the record is
correctly scoped to doctoral only.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **The deadline is not on the scholarship page.** It lives on the Key Dates page
  (`administration.jsp?labelID=4`). A future re-check of the scholarship URL alone
  will find no dates and may wrongly conclude the record has no deadline.
- **Timing:** the Spring 2027 window closes 14 October 2026. Two days after this
  check, `deadline_date` and `deadline_text` should roll to the Fall 2027 first round
  (4 December 2026), which opens 15 October 2026.
- **"Waived or reduced" is not a guaranteed full waiver.** The page's own wording
  leaves the fee waiver potentially partial, and the award includes no accommodation
  or airfare. It is not "fully funded" in the broad sense, and the record does not
  claim otherwise — but the card should not be read as a full-ride.
- **Terms changed for AY 115 onward.** The doctoral terms quoted above are the current
  ones; the page separately lists older rules for students enrolled AY 114 and before.
- **The university's own disclaimer:** "The scholarship is subject to change. Please
  refer to the admission brochure of that semester as accurate." The semester brochure
  therefore outranks this page, and the Chinese-only regulation PDF behind it was not
  machine-read in this check.
