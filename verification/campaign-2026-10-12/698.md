## Findings and exact quotes

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The scholarship is Posse's own programme, run on
the Foundation's site; the partner colleges provide the funding.

> "The Posse Foundation could not exist without its partner colleges and
> universities, who provide Posse Scholars with full-tuition scholarships, weekly
> faculty mentoring, and other support."

**Deadline — confirmed (no single date, exactly as the record records).** The
page publishes no national deadline and explicitly defers to city offices.

> "Depending on the Posse city or program, nomination timelines and deadlines may
> vary. Be sure to contact your local Posse office for details at the start of
> every calendar year."

> "Apply on time; depending on the Posse city or program, nominations are
> accepted in the spring and summer before senior year begins (contact your
> local Posse office for specific deadlines)"

The cycle is currently open: the About page says the Dynamic Assessment Process
runs "from September through December each year", so it is running now (12
October 2026) for fall 2027 entry. Nominators may nominate "as early as their
second semester junior year".

**Eligibility — confirmed, every element.** The record's summary matches the
page almost word for word:

> "To be eligible, a high school senior must:
> Be nominated by their high school or a community-based organization
> Be in the first term of their senior year in high school
> Demonstrate leadership within their high school, community or family
> Demonstrate academic potential
> Apply on time"

The recruitment-city requirement is confirmed on the same page (nominators work
with juniors/seniors "in one of Posse's 20-plus recruitment cities"). The page
also adds, consistently with the record: "The Posse Scholarship is neither a
minority nor a need-based scholarship. It is open to students of all
backgrounds."

**Funding — confirmed as "Full tuition", not fully funded.**

> "Each Posse Scholar wins a full-tuition scholarship to attend one of Posse's
> partner colleges or universities."

> "Posse Scholars receive full-tuition leadership scholarships from Posse's
> partner colleges and universities." (homepage)

Tuition only — the award does not cover living costs, room or board, so
describing the record as "Fully Funded" would be wrong. "Full tuition" is
accurate.

**Degree levels — confirmed as Bachelor's (undergraduate).** No page prints the
word "Bachelor's", but the level is unambiguous:

> "The four-year Campus Program works to ensure the retention of Posse Scholars
> and to increase the impact of the program on campus."

Eligibility is limited to high school seniors, the award funds study at a partner
college or university, and Posse reports graduation/first-generation-college
outcomes. Nothing on the site's recruiting pages offers a graduate award.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The record is accurate as it stands; nothing needs changing. Its
  `official_source_url` is live and is the right canonical page.
- There is **no date to publish** for a deadline. Any card showing a single Posse
  deadline would be fabricated: the only honest wording is "varies by Posse city
  or programme — contact your local Posse office", with nominations in spring and
  summer before senior year.
- Nomination is indirect: a student cannot apply directly; a registered high
  school or community-based organization must nominate them. The Posse Arts, Posse
  STEM and Posse Veterans streams run separate nomination processes not covered
  by this record.
- The award is worth full tuition at a partner college (the partner institutions
  provide it), not the wider cost of attendance.
- Minor site inconsistency, immaterial to this record: the nomination page says
  "20-plus recruitment cities" while the "Where We Recruit" page headline now
  reads "Posse recruits from 15-plus U.S. cities" (traditional cities listed:
  Atlanta, Bay Area, Boston, Chicago, D.C., Houston, Los Angeles, Miami, New
  Orleans, New York, Puerto Rico, plus Virtual Program cities). Worth watching
  at the next re-check.
