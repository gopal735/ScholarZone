## Findings

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is published by the OAS General Secretariat for a scholarship operated jointly with American University Washington College of Law: "The OAS General Secretariat (GS/OAS) is the central and permanent organ of the Organization of American States (OAS)." and "AUWCL and the OAS will select the scholarship awardees, based on the criteria listed above."

**Deadline — confirmed, cycle currently open.** The announcement states: "DEADLINES: Admission through AUWCL and scholarship application: Fall 2026: Residential LL.M. – May 1, 2026 Hybrid/Online LL.M. – June 1, 2026 Spring 2027: Residential LL.M. – November 1, 2026 Hybrid/Online LL.M. – November 30, 2026". On 12 October 2026 the Fall 2026 deadlines have passed; the open cycle is Spring 2027 — 1 November 2026 (residential/in-person) and 30 November 2026 (hybrid/online) — exactly matching the record's `deadline_label` and `deadline_date` (2026-11-01).

**Eligibility — confirmed.** "Be a citizen and/or permanent legal resident of any active OAS member state, except the United States."; "Applicants must hold a Juris Doctor (JD) from a US law school accredited by the American Bar Association (ABA) or a first degree in law from a foreign law school with equivalent standards."; "Be admitted to one of the LL.M programs offered by AUWCL"; "This scholarship opportunity is open to new applicants as well as current AUWCL students from an OAS member state, except the United States, and who are not a current OAS scholarship recipient." Ineligible: "Current OAS scholarship holders and Citizens and/or permanent residents of the United States. GS/OAS staff and their immediate relatives or GS/OAS consultants. OAS Permanent officials and their immediate relatives." Extra detail not in the record: Argentina applicants must submit a required form (NOTA-SOLICITUD-DGCIN) through the National Liaison Office (ONE).

**Funding — confirmed.** "BENEFITS: 50% Tuition waiver". Not fully funded, and the record correctly says "Tuition Fee Waiver". The page's own table lists total tuition USD 61,944 with "Cost to be covered by the scholar USD 30,972", and expenses not covered include: "Remaining 50% of tuition fees; Travel expenses (to and from the country of study), if applicable; Mandatory university fees; Cost of books and study materials; Health insurance; Graduation expenses; Visa expenses (where applicable); and any other costs or additional living and study cost not covered by the scholarship."

**Degree levels — confirmed.** All programmes are LL.M. (Master of Laws), i.e. Master's level: "PROGRAMS: LL.M in International Human Rights and Humanitarian Law; LL.M. en Derechos Humanos y Derecho Humanitario; LL.M. in International Arbitration and Business Law" — offered onsite, hybrid, or online, in English (and Spanish for the HRHL LL.M.).

## Before trusting the record

3. Applicants must first be admitted by AUWCL; selected awardees are published in August and December at www.oas.org/scholarships.
