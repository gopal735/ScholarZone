## What checks out

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is the awarding organisation's own (ersnet.org), not an aggregator or a news item, and it names ERS as the funder. The Swiss country in the record is also right: the guidance letterhead reads "ERS Headquarters / 4 Avenue Ste-Luce / CH1003 Switzerland".

> "European Respiratory Society (ERS) Visiting Mid-Career Investigator Travel (VISIT) Grants offer mid-career investigators, clinicians and allied healthcare professionals who are transitioning to more established stages of their careers to visit an institution(s) outside of their home country for up to one month."

**Deadline — confirmed, and the cycle is open today.** The page states 31 October 2026, which matches the record's `2026-10-31`, and the call is live (opened 13 July 2026; portfolio table status "Open").

> "Applications for the ERS VISIT Grant are open." … "Application deadline: 31 October, 2026" … "Notification of application decision: Mid-February, 2027"

> "The deadline for submitting applications is 31 October 2026 at 23:59 pm CET." — guidance PDF

**Funding — confirmed as partial.** No tuition, no stipend; a fixed travel allowance plus a per-diem.

> "Travel allowance €600 for mobility between European countries (including both EU and non-EU states) / €2'000 for mobility from Europe to non-Europe, or non-Europe to Europe (long distance flight) / Accommodation / meals allowance €200 (fixed amount per day of stay and travel)" … "No additional costs will be considered."

A tuition-only award is not fully funded, and this one is further from "fully funded" still — it is explicitly not a study award:

> "ERS VISIT programme is not meant to exchange expertise regarding teaching or science and, therefore, does not cover undergraduate studies and is not designed solely to cover the costs of attending workshops, courses, conferences or congresses."

**Eligibility — partly confirmed.** The mobility and duration claims match ("exchanges between two institutions within the same country are not allowed"; visit "from one week, up to a maximum of one month"), and there is no nationality bar ("All nationalities, European and non-European based applicants may submit a proposal").

## What a person must decide before trusting this record

**1. `degree_levels: "Postdoctoral"` is not supported.** This is a travel/mobility grant, not a degree programme, and its target group is mid-career, not postdoctoral. ERS's own portfolio table lists the VISIT applicants profile as "PhD, MD(Resp), or equivalent" with duration "Up to 1" month, and the guidance requires:

> "The application for ERS VISIT grant is open to those within 5 years of appointment to clinical or academic equivalent of Associate Professor / Reader level (taking into consideration any work hiatus period which will be deducted)."

> "Applicants holding a professor title are not eligible to apply."

Someone holding a professor title is not a postdoc. A reviewer should decide whether to relabel the record (e.g. as a mid-career mobility/travel grant) rather than leave "Postdoctoral" on the card.

**2. The eligibility summary over-generalises the degree rule and omits two hard requirements.** The record says all three named groups must hold "a PhD, an MD in respiratory medicine or an equivalent qualification". The guidance splits them:

> "Clinicians and researchers must hold a postgraduate degree such as PhD, MD(Res) or equivalent."

> "Allied healthcare professionals must hold a minimum of an MSc, or equivalent degree at the time of the application (e.g. BSc degree with evidence of continuous specialised training in the respiratory health)."

Two requirements that would disqualify most applicants are missing from the record entirely:

> "Applicants must be ERS active members for at least one year when applying for the ERS Mid-Career Investigator travel grant."

> "I have not worked at the host institution and have not lived in the host country in the 12 months prior to the application deadline." — application form declaration

Also note the application form's own declarations, which a reader of the catalogue card will not see: "I am within 5 years of appointment to clinical or academic equivalent of Associate Professor / Reader level (taking into account any career breaks)" and "I do not hold a Professor title".
