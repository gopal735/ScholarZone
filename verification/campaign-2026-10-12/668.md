## Why `needs_review` and not `active`

Everything the record asserts about the awarding body, the open rolling call, the eligibility rules and the partial funding is confirmed word-for-word by COMSTECH's own page. The one thing that does not hold is the degree-level claim.

The record says `PhD`. The page does not support that: its summary block states a minimum qualification of `MS/Ph.D.`, its call details apply separate age limits to "Master's studies" (35) and "doctoral studies" (45), and its eligibility section goes further and accepts a Bachelor's degree with five years of professional experience. The page is also inconsistent with itself on this point, so the claim cannot be confirmed rather than being cleanly contradicted — which is why the verdict is `needs_review` rather than `conflicting`. The award is a 3–6 month research placement at a CCoE host institution, not a degree programme.

## What a person should know before trusting this record

1. **`degree_levels: "PhD"` is too narrow.** Anyone filtering for PhD-only opportunities will get the wrong picture: Master's-level applicants, and Bachelor's holders with five years' experience, are also eligible. This needs a human decision on the correct value.
2. **The record's `eligibility_summary` omits two hard gates** stated on the page: the age limits (35 for MS/Ph.D. holders, 45 for post-doc) and the named 21-country OIC least-developed-country list. The deadline label likewise omits neither — it matches, but a reader should see the age caps.
3. **The record's `region: "Africa"` understates the scope.** Eligibility is "All OIC member states", spanning Africa, the Middle East and Asia.
4. **The deadline is genuinely rolling, so it will never "close" in a way this record can date.** The page says applications are accepted yearly and the call is currently open; the related flyer PDF was behind a login wall and was not read, so a date published only there remains unverified.
5. **Funding is partial.** COMSTECH pays airfare and visa fees only; accommodation and subsistence come from the host. The record's "Partially Funded" is correct and should not be upgraded to "Fully Funded".
