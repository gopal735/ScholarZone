## What a person should know before trusting the record

- Every claim in the catalogue record (awarding body, deadline date and time, eligibility, fully funded, non-degree six-week exchange) matches the live official sources. The record can move to `active`.
- **The deadline expires tomorrow.** The 2027 cycle opened 22 September 2026 and closes 13 October 2026 at 16:00/4:00 PM GMT. Any applicant-facing card should surface this immediately.
- The exact 16:00 GMT cut-off is published only in the 2027 application instructions PDF, not on the `/apply` page (which shows the calendar date alone) — the next re-check should read both.
- "Fully Funded" is accurate but means *all programme costs* (visa support, round-trip travel, the six-week institute, limited accident/sickness cover, housing and meals, optional PDE). The U.S. Government does **not** cover Fellows' salaries while away from work or personal purchases — standard exclusions for a fully funded exchange programme, not a partial award.
- After 13 October 2026 the site rolls over to the next cycle; all dates must be re-read then.
