# Verification memo — record 147

**International Industrial Talents Education Special Program (INTENSE Program)** (Taiwan)
Checked live on **12 October 2026** (ISO 2026-10-12T19:30:00Z).

## Verdict: `conflicting` — confidence: high

The programme is real, currently running, and almost everything in the record checked
out exactly — awarding body, every deadline window, the fully-funded claim, and the
degree levels. The one defect: the record's eligibility summary says Chinese-taught
programmes require **TOCFL Level B1**, but every official page read puts **TOCFL A2**
as the admission requirement (B1 only later, to keep the scholarship). Because the
brief requires eligibility to agree before a record can be `active`, the verdict is
`conflicting` on that single, demonstrable point.

## Exact quotes that carry the verdict

**Awarding body — confirmed**
- MOE: "The Ministry of Education (MOE) has launched the \"International Industrial
  Talents Education Special Program (INTENSE Program)\" for talents coming to Taiwan
  for study and employment after graduation."
- Taipei Tech OIA: "The INTENSE Program is an industry-academia collaboration
  initiative launched by Taiwan's Ministry of Education."
- KMU: "This program is approved by the Ministry of Education (MOE), Taiwan."
- NDC/company roles per NCHU: "The National Development Council (NDC) provides
  industry-university scholarships… Participating companies provide: Monthly living
  stipend of at least NTD 10,000 per student."

**Deadline — confirmed (all four windows in the record, verbatim)**
- NCHU: "Application: Sep. 10, 2026, 10:00 AM – Oct. 12, 2026, 5:00 PM (GMT+8)"
  (the Semiconductor programme has a second window: Oct. 6 – Oct. 30, 2026).
- NCU: "the application period running from October 1 to October 22, 2026".
- KMU: "Application Period: September 21, 2026 9:00 ~ October 23, 2026 17:00".
- NTUT: "Student nomination deadline: March 10, 2027" (2027 Fall intake).
- The MOE page itself publishes no deadline, consistent with the record's "no single
  INTENSE deadline" wording. On 12 Oct 2026 the NCHU window closes today and the NCU
  and KMU windows were still open — the cycles checked are genuinely current.

**Eligibility — contradicted (the one failing claim)**
- NCHU language table: "Chinese-taught | TOCFL A2+ | TOCFL B1 by the 3rd semester"
  (columns: *Application Requirement | Continued Scholarship Requirement*).
- NCHU FAQ: "Applicants for English-taught programs must have at least CEFR B1 level,
  while those applying for Chinese-taught programs must hold at least TOCFL A2."
- Confirmed from the same page: "Must meet the MOE Regulations Regarding
  International Students Undertaking Studies in Taiwan."
- Confirmed nationality targeting from NCU: "the Advanced Mechanical Engineering
  Program (open to applicants from Vietnam, Indonesia, Philippines, and Thailand)".
- Not confirmable from any page read: the record's "some programmes open to students
  of all nationalities".

**Funding — confirmed fully funded (not tuition-only)**
- MOE: "The Taiwanese government covers tuition fees and other administrative costs
  for up to two years. Participating companies provide each student with a living
  allowance at least NTD10,000 per month and an internship allowance (above or equal
  minimum wage)."
- KMU: "Recipients will receive a monthly living allowance of NT$10,000. In addition,
  tuition and miscellaneous fees, initial administrative expenses upon first arrival
  in Taiwan, and one-time airfare will also be covered."
- NCU: "full tuition and credit waivers for up to two years, a one-way flight ticket
  to Taiwan, initial administrative expense support, and a monthly stipend of NT$10,000
  sponsored by industry partners".

**Degree levels — confirmed Master's and Bachelor's**
- KMU: "…to welcome international students to pursue a Master's degree at KMU,
  Taiwan." NCHU: "Department of Mechanical Engineering Master's Degree - English".
- Taiwan Tech publishes INTENSE guidelines for an "Undergraduate program (Bachelor)"
  as well as graduate programmes.

## What a person should know before trusting this record

1. **The deadline field is trustworthy and unusually well-researched** — all four
   windows match the universities' own pages exactly. But windows move every cycle:
   re-check each partner university's own INTENSE/admissions page, never a central
   page (MOE publishes none).
2. **Fix the eligibility wording.** The accurate statement is: Chinese-taught
   programmes require **TOCFL A2 at admission**, with **TOCFL B1 by the 3rd semester**
   only to continue the scholarship; English-taught programmes require **CEFR B1 at
   admission**. The record's "Chinese-taught … TOCFL Level B1" overstates the entry
   requirement.
3. **The recorded `official_source_url` is the wrong page.** `gocfs.ntu.edu.tw` is a
   National Taiwan University student-affairs division homepage with no INTENSE
   content. The recommended canonical URL is the MOE programme page
   (https://english.moe.gov.tw/cp-48-39791-964aa-1.html); programme-level application
   detail lives on each participating university's admissions page.
4. Minor: at least one Taiwan-Vietnam base track describes doctoral-level INTENSE
   offerings; the record lists only Master's and Bachelor's. Both listed levels are
   genuinely offered, but a reviewer may want to add Doctoral.
5. Nothing about the funding claim needs changing: tuition (capped, up to 2 years),
   monthly living stipend (≥ NT$10,000), one-way airfare and pre-arrival
   administrative costs are all covered, with a post-graduation work obligation in
   Taiwan matching the funded duration.
