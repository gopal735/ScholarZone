## Claim-by-claim

- **Awarding body — confirmed.** Both pages read are UNHCR's own and present DAFI as
  UNHCR's programme, begun as a German initiative in 1992:
  > "UNHCR's Tertiary Refugee Scholarship Programme (DAFI) offers qualified refugee and
  > returnee (in selected countries\*) students the possibility to earn an undergraduate degree
  > in their country of asylum or home country."

  > "Since 1992, the German Albert Einstein Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) has been
  > supporting young refugees around the world access or continue higher education."

  The record's donor detail (Denmark, Czech Republic, private donors) is not confirmed on
  any page read in full — a minor detail, not a material contradiction.
- **Deadline — unconfirmed as an open cycle.** The record correctly says there is no single
  global deadline: each UNHCR country operation publishes its own window on its Help country
  page. But every window found is past:
  > "Application Period: 7 July – 31 July 2026 (until 23:59 Bishkek time)." (Kyrgyzstan)

  > "Applications will be accepted from 15 July to 7 August 2026. Please note that late
  > applications will not be considered." (Rwanda)

  Kazakhstan and Tajikistan closed 31 July 2026; a partner-posted Yemen call closed
  14 September 2026. No currently-open call exists today. Note also that the record's
  Kyrgyzstan example (21 August 2026) does not match the current Kyrgyzstan page
  (31 July 2026).
- **Eligibility — confirmed.**
  > "DAFI covers programmes at International Standard Classification of Education (ISCED)
  > level 6, bachelor's degrees or equivalent level qualifications. ... DAFI can also cover
  > short-cycle tertiary education, ISCED level 5 courses. ... Applicants currently benefiting
  > from another Higher Education scholarship programmes, are not eligible to apply for DAFI
  > scholarship."

  Refugees and, in selected countries, returnees; undergraduate or 2+ year TVET; country of
  asylum or home country; no other higher-education scholarship. The pages add criteria the
  record omits (age 28 or younger at the start of studies, one DAFI family member at a time,
  financial need, completed secondary school) — omissions, not contradictions.
- **Funding — confirmed as Fully Funded.** It is a full support package, not tuition-only:
  > "The scholarship package varies from country to country and covers a range of costs
  > including tuition and study materials, food, transportation, and accommodation." (Rwanda)

  > "The DAFI Scholarship covers tuition fees. Scholars also receive a monthly living stipend
  > and one-time allowance for educational materials." (Kyrgyzstan)
- **Degree levels — confirmed, undergraduate only.**
  > "DAFI scholarships are awarded for a maximum of four years of undergraduate or equivalent
  > degree studies at accredited higher education institutions. The programme does not
  > support master's degree studies."

## What a person should know before trusting this record

1. **The record's data is sound.** Awarding body, eligibility, fully-funded coverage and
   undergraduate-only levels all check out against UNHCR's own pages.
2. **It is not an open cycle today.** DAFI is a rolling, per-country programme with no global
   deadline; the 2026/2027 calls all closed over the summer. Do not display it as a
   currently-open application; the honest presentation is "check your UNHCR country
   operation for the next call".
3. **Replace the source URL.** The recorded `official_source_url` is the application portal
   (JavaScript-gated, no programme terms), not UNHCR's own page. Point the record at the
   global DAFI page: https://www.unhcr.org/what-we-do/build-better-futures/education/higher-education-and-skills/dafi-tertiary-scholarship-0
4. **Two loose ends for a human with a browser.** UNHCR's DAFI FAQ page
   (services.unhcr.org/opportunities/frequently-asked-questions/dafi-scholarship-programme-faqs)
   is JavaScript-rendered and could not be read here; its content (age cap of 28; partial
   sponsorship possible where a candidate has other funding) is known only from search
   snippets and is worth confirming and possibly folding into the eligibility summary. The
   Czech Republic donor detail likewise could not be confirmed.
