**Eligibility — confirmed, with one addition.** "are a citizen of a country outside of the EU/EEA and Switzerland; have applied for a full programme at Umeå University* (Bachelor's or Master's); have completed your application for programme studies before the application deadline on 15 January 2026; have paid the application fee by 2 February 2026**; and have chosen a Umeå University programme as priority 1 on the University Admissions website by 15 January 2026 at the latest." The FAQ adds: "Umeå University does not offer any other forms of financial support. We recommend that you look for external funding if needed."

**Degree levels — confirmed.** "have applied for a full programme at Umeå University* (Bachelor's or Master's)" — bachelor's and master's, as recorded.
