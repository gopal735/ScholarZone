# Verification memo — record 684, Mauritius-Africa Scholarship Scheme (MASS)

**Campaign:** 2026-10-12 · **Task:** 79/100 · **Checked:** 12 October 2026, 19:22 UTC

## Verdict

**`conflicting`** — confidence **`high`**

The official page was found and read in full. Four of the record's five claims
(awarding body, eligibility, funding, degree levels) match the awarding body's
own documents exactly. The deadline claim does not: the record says *"No central
date is published"*, but the Ministry's own Call for Applications publishes
**27 March 2026** for the 2026 edition — and that date has already passed.
There is no 2027 edition yet, so the record currently describes a closed
scheme, not an open one. Per the brief, a past deadline means the record cannot
be marked active.

## The quotes that carry the verdict

**Deadline — the contradictory claim.** The record asserts no central date
exists. The Call says:

> Such Nominating Agency, which may typically be part of the Ministry of
> Education in the country of the applicant, will then shortlist and endorse the
> applications of a maximum of FIFTEEN (15) eligible candidates … and submit the
> duly completed application forms by **27 March 2026**

The Nominating Agency Guidelines restate it as a fixed central date:

> Nominating Agencies shall submit the list of applicants recommended for
> selection for the July/September 2026 intake by the set deadline of
> **27 March 2026**.

The applicant-facing guideline is the only place the record's wording comes from,
and it applies to the applicant's own submission, not to the Ministry's deadline:

> This should be done by a deadline to be determined by the Nominating Agency
> and communicated to prospective applicants.

**Awarding body — confirmed.**

> Applications and all supporting documents must be forwarded to the Ministry of
> Tertiary Education, Science and Research of the Republic of Mauritius at the
> address mentioned below for the final selection.

> The final decision for the award of a scholarship rests with the Ministry.

**Eligibility — confirmed, every distinct claim.**

> The Government of Mauritius invites applications from deserving students who
> are resident citizens of member states of the African Union or African
> Commonwealth countries for the Mauritius Africa Scholarship Scheme, as per the
> following criteria: 1. For undergraduate programmes, applicants should be above
> 18 years of age and should not have reached their 26th birthday by
> 01 January 2027. 2. For MPhil, MPhil/PhD and PhD programmes, applicants should
> not have reached their 40th birthday by 01 January 2027.

> Applicants must have already applied for a full-time on-campus programme
> (Diploma, Degree, MPhil or PhD) at a public Higher Education Institution in
> Mauritius … for the academic year starting in 2026.

> Candidates must be resident citizens of member states of the African Union or
> African Commonwealth countries. Mauritian nationals are NOT eligible to apply
> under this Scheme. — *Guidelines for Applicants, §1.1*

The 60% / 12/20 threshold checks out for both levels:

> at least an overall average of 60% or an overall average of 12/20 equivalent to
> A level — *undergraduate degree, UG Guidelines §1.3.2*

> A recognized undergraduate degree with at least an upper second class degree
> (or CPA / GPA greater than 60%) — *MPhil and MPhil/PhD, PG Guidelines §1.3.2–1.3.3*

**Funding — "Partially Funded" confirmed.**

> The Scholarship will support successful candidates in meeting their tuition
> fees and contributing to their living expenses during their studies in
> Mauritius. Furthermore, the scholarship covers return airfare by the most
> economical route.

The capped tuition and explicitly partial stipend, from the Guidelines:

> This scholarship provides for an annual payment of fees and other
> studies-related costs … as per the grid below: 1 SADC Countries Local Fees
> MUR100,000 2 Non-SADC Countries International Fees MUR160,000

> Fees and other studies-related costs over the amounts mentioned above shall be
> borne by the awardee.

> A monthly living allowance of MUR 16,500 will be paid to awardees as part
> support to meet their living expenses. Students are expected to supplement
> this amount to cover the cost of living in Mauritius.

Not fully funded, and not tuition-only — airfare is included, but as a
reimbursement ("Awardees must make their own arrangements to pay for their
one-way air tickets and then apply for a refund upon arrival in Mauritius").

**Degree levels — confirmed.**

> 1 Undergraduate Diploma Three (3) years 2 Undergraduate Degree Four (4) years
> 3 MPhil Two (2) years + 6 months for thesis defence 4 MPhil / PhD Three (3)
> years + 6 months for approval of transfer report and One (1) year + 6 months
> for thesis defence 5 PhD Three (3) years + 6 months for thesis defence

## What a person should know before trusting this record

- **The scheme is closed as of today.** The 2026 edition ran roughly October 2025
  to March 2026. The HEI application windows in Section 8 of the 2026 Guidelines
  (University of Mauritius, UTM, ADI, UDM, Mahatma Gandhi Institute, Rabindranath
  Tagore Institute, Polytechnics Mauritius) all closed between **27 February
  2026 and end-March 2026**, for an intake stated to begin August/October 2026.
  Nothing is open on 12 October 2026.
- **A 2027 call should appear from late October 2026**, on the pattern the
  Ministry repeats each year (the 2025 edition is archived at
  `…/tertiaryeducation/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Call-for-Application-2025.pdf`,
  also with a March deadline). The live MASS page is where it will land.
- **`deadline_label` is wrong in a way that matters.** It says no central date is
  published; the Ministry publishes one every edition. Anyone reading the label
  would wrongly conclude the scheme has no deadline at all.
- **`degree_levels` is missing the combined MPhil/PhD route**, which the Call
  lists as a distinct programme with its own duration.
- **Suggested canonical URL:** `https://tertiaryeducation.govmu.org/te/index.php/mass/`.
  The recorded URL is a 2026-edition PDF that will be replaced by a new path
  when the 2027 call is posted; the programme page is stable across editions and
  is the page the Call itself directs applicants to.
- **One caveat on my evidence.** I read PDFs by text extraction, which inserts
  stray spaces inside words (e.g. "Af rican", "th e", "du ring"). The quotes
  above are reproduced as the page actually reads visually; I did not invent or
  guess any text. I could not use web search (the search provider returned an
  allowance error), so the "no 2027 edition" finding rests on reading the live
  Ministry pages directly rather than on a search index — which is stronger
  evidence anyway.
