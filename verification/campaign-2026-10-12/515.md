## Quotes that carry the verdict

- **Awarding body:** "Scholarship (for International Students Only) : Japanese Government (Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology [MEXT])" (GRIPS page); "3. Host University National Graduate Institute for Policy Studies (GRIPS)" (MEXT 2027 guidelines).
- **Deadline:** "Recruitment will be conducted through the recommending authorities of each eligible country. Please ask the Japanese Embassy for further information." (MEXT 2027 guidelines) — no central deadline exists, and "A complete set of your required application documents must reach your recommending authority by the designated deadline." Deadlines are set per country by each recommending authority (usually the Japanese embassy).
- **Eligibility:** "Applicants must be, in principle, under 40 years of age as of October 1, 2027 (i.e. born on or after October 2, 1987)." … "Applicants must hold a Bachelor's degree or equivalent from a recognized/accredited university or college, and have achieved excellent academic performance." … "At least 3 years of full-time work experience in total as of October 1, 2027, in public administration (preferably 5 years or more)." (MEXT 2027 guidelines; the under-40 and 3-years-experience rules are repeated in the GRIPS brochure.)
- **Funding:** "It is fully supported, including full scholarships for all students, by the Japanese government." (GRIPS brochure) — MEXT 2027 guidelines itemise: "Allowance: 242,000 yen per month."; economy-class airfare to and from Japan; "Education Fees: Fees for the entrance examination, matriculation and tuition are waivered."; accommodation at the Tokyo International Exchange Center. This is genuinely fully funded, not tuition-only.
- **Degree levels:** "Degrees Offered: Master of Public Policy" (GRIPS page); "Course duration is one year, resulting in the conferral of a Master's Degree of Public Policy by GRIPS." (MEXT 2027 guidelines).

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- Every claim in the catalogue record (MEXT as awarding body hosted at GRIPS; no central deadline; under-40-in-principle, graduate, public-administrator with work-experience eligibility; fully funded; master's level) was confirmed verbatim on the awarding bodies' own pages on 12 October 2026.
- The record's "varies" deadline precision is correct and unavoidable: MEXT publishes no central date; each recommending authority (embassy or designated nominating body) sets its own designated deadline, so the card cannot honestly carry a single date.
- The cycle currently advertised is the **2027 intake** (commencement October 2027; scholarship period October 2027–September 2028). MEXT's English guidelines for it were published 2026-08-13, so the pages are current, not stale.
- The monthly allowance is 242,000 yen (FY2026 figure) and MEXT notes the amount may change with the government budget; the age limit for the 2027 cycle is specifically "under 40 as of October 1, 2027 (born on or after October 2, 1987)".
- Scope note for editors: "Young Leaders' Program" also exists in four other courses under other universities (Healthcare Administration at Nagoya University, Business Administration at Hitotsubashi, Law at Kyushu University). This record concerns only the GRIPS School of Government / School of Local Governance course, which matches the record's stated awarding body; no change needed, but future re-checks should not mix in the other universities' YLP courses.
