## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The record's "Partially Funded" label is accurate: the page itself calls the award "supplementary financial assistance", up to a maximum of AUD $75,000 paid in semester instalments, subject to academic progress. It is not fully funded.
- The scholarship is not restricted to Cambridge/Oxford/etc. despite the partner institutions listed on the page — "Other" and "any recognised overseas university" are explicit, matching the record.
- Applications require a Confirmation of Aboriginality (common-sealed letter from a Land Council or incorporated Aboriginal/Torres Strait Islander organisation) and a budget; applications without it are marked incomplete.
- The portal is a Power Apps page that rendered fully on this check; the fetch displayed a benign "read only version of the page" banner. If a future automated check sees only that shell, re-fetch or use the site's Scholarship Search rather than treating the URL as dead.
