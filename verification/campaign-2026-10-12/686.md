## Before you trust the record

- The award is a **20% tuition waiver only** (partially funded). It covers no
  living costs; recipients must still meet full fee-paying financial
  obligations.
- There is **no application to submit**: assessment is automatic on
  application for admission, so there is no deadline date to track.
- The page is versioned by commencement year. The record is correct for the
  2027 cycle; when the University rolls the page over to a 2028 version, the
  title and amount wording should be re-checked.
- Scholarship values are advised in the Letter of Offer, and the number of
  scholarships (500) is indicative only.
- Small nuance: the record states applicants must not hold another University
  scholarship; the official page permits it with prior special dispensation.
