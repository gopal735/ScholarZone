## What the record claims vs. what the pages say

- **Awarding body:** confirmed. The NSP lists the scheme under "All India Council For Technical Education" with the title "AICTE - Pragati Scholarship Scheme For Girl Students ( Technical Degree) (Merit Based Scheme)", and the guidelines state the "Scheme will be implemented and monitored by the AICTE."
- **Deadline:** confirmed for a cycle open today (12 Oct 2026). The NSP entry states: "Scheme Open from : 01-06-2026 Student Application Open till : 31-10-2026 Defective Application Verification Open till:15-11-2026 Institute Verification Open till:15-11-2026 DNO/SNO/MNO Verification Open till:30-11-2026". This matches the record's "31 October 2026 for student applications for academic year 2026-27".
- **Eligibility:** confirmed. Guidelines, section 2.0: "1) The girl candidate should be admitted to First year of Degree level course OR Second year of Degree level course through lateral entry in any of the AICTE approved Institution of respective year. 2) Maximum two girl child per family are eligible. 3) Family income from all sources should not be more than Rs. 8 lakh per annum during the current financial year." Renewal requires progression: "Student failing to get promoted to the next class/level would forfeit the scholarship."
- **Funding:** confirmed as partially funded. Guidelines, section 4.0: "Rs. 50,000/- per annum for every year of study i.e. maximum 4 years for first year admitted students and maximum 3 years for second year admitted students through lateral entry as lump sum amount towards payment of college fee, purchase of computer, stationeries, books, equipments, softwares etc. No other additional grant will be payable in lieu of hostel charges and medical charges etc."
- **Degree levels:** confirmed as undergraduate degree courses (the NSP lists the Diploma variant as a separate scheme). Selection is "solely on the basis of merit of qualifying examination to peruse the Technical Degree course from any of the AICTE approved institutions i.e. in First year or Second year (through lateral entry only)".

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The record's `official_source_url` (scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships) is a valid official government page but is a generic portal listing; the scheme-specific official page is the Specifications PDF linked from it (recommended above).
- The scheme is an annual cycle: a fresh application window typically opens in June and closes 31 October; the 2026-27 window closed applications on 31-10-2026 (verification ran to 15-11 and 30-11-2026). Re-check the NSP listing in June 2027 for the next cycle.
- "Partially Funded" is accurate: Rs 50,000 per annum lump sum covers fees, books, equipment etc., with hostel and medical charges explicitly excluded. Payment is by DBT from AICTE.
- The linked guidelines PDF is dated 2020-21 but remains the standing specification referenced by the NSP page; watch for a newer revision at the next check.
