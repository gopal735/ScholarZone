## Claim-by-claim adjudication

### Awarding body — confirmed
The page is on `wi.org.nz`, the New Zealand Federation of Women's Institutes'
own domain, and presents the awards as coming from the Foundation; the
conditions document gives the trust's postal address:

> "Scholarships from the Barbara Wood Memorial Foundation"
> "Barbara Wood Memorial Foundation Trust / P O Box 137 / WELLINGTON 6140"

Matches the record's "Barbara Wood Memorial Foundation, administered by the New
Zealand Federation of Women's Institutes".

### Deadline — confirmed, currently open cycle
> "Applications close on 1 February 2027 - ensure your applicatIon has covered
> all criteria requested."

and, from the conditions document:

> "Applications must reach the Secretary by 3.00 p.m. Monday 1 February 2027 on
> the official application form (BWM 1)."

The page also states "Applications for the 2026 academic year have closed", so
the 2027 round is the live cycle today. Matches the record's `1 February 2027`
/ `2027-02-01` exactly.

### Eligibility — confirmed (verbatim match)
> "The scholarships are available to tertiary students, both female and male,
> who were born in the South Pacific region which not only includes the island
> nations of the Pacific but also New Zealand and Australia. Students born in
> the South Pacific wishing to study in New Zealand or anywhere in the South
> Pacific are eligible for the scholarship."

Every element of the catalogue's eligibility summary (any gender, birthplace in
the South Pacific, study in NZ or the South Pacific) is on the page.

### Funding — confirmed partial
> "Scholarships up to $1,000 a year are granted depending on the need and cost
> of the course being undertaken and are granted at the discretion of the
> Trustees of the Barbara Wood Memorial Foundation."

Up to $1,000/year is a small, need-based contribution, not full funding — the
catalogue's "Partially Funded" is correct. The conditions reinforce this: they
require "A full budget for the total cost of the course" and "The amount of
financial contribution which you are prepared to make".

### Degree levels — confirmed
The page targets "tertiary students" and the conditions require an "accredited
educational or training course"; the published 2026 successful applicants were
all at Diploma or Bachelor's level ("Bachelor of Applied Social Work",
"Diploma of Mental Health and Addictions", "Diploma of Veterinarian Nursing",
"Bachelor of Midwifery", "Bachelor of Science"), consistent with the record's
"Diploma, Bachelor's". Nothing on the page contradicts it.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The award is genuinely small: up to **$1,000 a year**, at the Trustees'
  discretion. It should never appear next to "fully funded" language.
- The deadline **rolls annually on 1 February**. This verification covers the
  2027 cycle; after 1 February 2027 the record must be re-checked against the
  same page for the 2028 wording.
- Application is by the official form (BWM 1) plus the conditions listed in
  BWM 2, submitted to `exec@wi.org.nz` or Barbara Wood Memorial Foundation
  Trust, P O Box 137, Wellington 6140; awards are notified by 31 March.
- Minor caveat: "Diploma, Bachelor's" is inferred from the 2026 recipient list;
  the published conditions speak of an "accredited educational or training
  course" generally, so a cautious editor could broaden the wording to
  "tertiary (recent awards at Diploma/Bachelor's level)".
