> "La FCM UNCUYO prevé la adjudicación de 20 becas por un monto de $151.637,14 cada una. La

> "La convocatoria incorpora además la posibilidad de otorgar avales académicos a estudiantes

**Degree levels** — confirmed in substance as undergraduate study:

> "Esta nota deberá ser completada por estudiantes regulares universitarios/as de grado y

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **This is an internal award, not an international one.** Only students already enrolled and
  regular (i.e. in good standing) at FCM UNCUYO can apply. Anyone browsing ScholarZone for an
  Argentine scholarship to study abroad should not read this as open to them — the record's own
  `eligibility_summary` says so, but the label "Argentina / Latin America" on a listing page can
  easily mislead.
- **Awarding unit is a departmental directorate**, not the university as a whole: the Dirección
  de Investigación y Relaciones Institucionales within the Secretaría de Desarrollo Institucional
  of FCM. The record's `awarding_body` wording matches this.
- **The award is a research-training stipend, not a scholarship in the "covers your studies"
  sense.** ARS 151,637.14 per student for 210 clock-hours of supervised project work, with
  awards decided in December 2026 and the work starting 1 February 2027. It is not "Fully
  Funded" and includes no tuition benefit.
- **Two of the 20 places are reserved** by field (one Enfermería, one Escuela de Técnicos
  Asistenciales en Salud), conditional on reaching 60 points. Worth knowing if the catalogue
  ever surfaces the number of awards.
- **Only one caveat on wording, not on substance:** the record's `degree_levels` is "Bachelor's",
  while the official text says "grado y pregrado", which also covers the non-degree tertiary
  Técnicos Asistenciales en Salud programmes. Widen the label; do not downgrade the record.
- **The stipend amount and the reserved places live only in the bases PDF**, not on the HTML
  pages. I read the PDF as extracted text; the amount is quoted above verbatim from it.
- **Cycle timing for the next re-check:** this call closes to students on 20 November 2026.
  Re-check after mid-December 2026 for the award list, and around September–October 2027 for
  the 2027–2028 call.
