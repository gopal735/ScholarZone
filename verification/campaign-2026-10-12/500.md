## What a person should know before trusting this record

- **The deadline is announced but not yet open.** At check time (12 Oct 2026) the first window (15 Dec 2026 – 15 Jan 2027) opens in two months; the page also lists a second window (15 Jun – 15 Jul 2027). Applications before 15 Dec 2026 are not possible.
- **The site contradicts itself on amounts, mildly.** The overview page says max 885 € (study) / 1.650 € (doctoral); the detailed "Finanzielle Förderung" page says max 855 € / 1.750 €. The record claims no amounts, so the verdict is unaffected, but a future re-check should take the figure from the detail page.
- **B2 German is a hard gate**: applications from international students without proof of German skills "werden nicht berücksichtigt".
- **Route matters**: applicants in HSS project countries apply through the local HSS Auslandsbüro; everyone else applies directly through the online portal (https://stipendienbewerbung.hss.de/).
- The FAQ notes doctoral/graduate scholars funded from Federal Foreign Office funds follow separate guidelines and rates, so future doctoral figures may differ from the general Promotionsförderung rates.
