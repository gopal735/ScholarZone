## What a person should know before trusting this record

1. **The record's "from outside Canada" wording is too narrow.** Nothing on Perimeter's
   pages restricts PSI to non-Canadians. The financial-support page explicitly addresses
   **"Canadian PSI applicants"** and tells them to apply to NSERC's Canada Graduate
   Research Scholarship before 1 December, a Canadian alumnus is quoted on the landing
   page, and the site advertises admission of "students from countries around the world".
   I recommend rewording the eligibility summary to "open to applicants worldwide,
   including Canada"; as written it could wrongly turn Canadian readers away.

2. **"Fully Funded" is correct but not a blanket guarantee.** The page says
   **"Scholarships are extended to up to all accepted students"**, and that
   **"Consideration for a full scholarship is part of the admissions process."**
   The provisions also **"have no cash value and are provided as-is."**

3. **Two different deadlines appear on Perimeter's own site.** The FAQ page still reads
   **"Applications received by February 1, 2026 at 11:59pm EST will receive first
   consideration"** — a leftover from the previous cycle. The How to Apply page and the
   AcademicJobsOnline listing both give January 24, 2027 for the 2027 intake, and the FAQ
   date is in the past, so I treated January 24, 2027 as authoritative. The awarding body
   should refresh that FAQ line; it is the only genuinely contradictory material I found.

4. **The deadline is a first-consideration date, not a hard cut-off.** Late applications
   **"will be considered until all positions are filled"**, and approximately 25 positions
   are offered against several hundred applications.

5. **The degree is granted by the University of Waterloo, not Perimeter.** Perimeter runs
   the programme and award, and issues a PSI certificate. The record's `awarding_body` is
   correct for the scholarship, but applicants should know which institution confers the
   MSc.
