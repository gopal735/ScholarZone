## Findings

- **Awarding body — confirmed.** The page sits on `griffith.edu.au` and the programme is Griffith University's own:
  > "The International Academic Merit Scholarship recognises international students with strong academic results applying for undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs at Griffith University."

- **Deadline — confirmed (rolling, no closing date).** The page publishes no application deadline at all, because there is nothing to apply for; the scholarship is assessed against the student's application to study:
  > "You don't need to apply for the scholarship, we've kept it as easy as possible. Just apply to study at Griffith and we will let you know if you have been successful."

  Eligibility is governed by *commencement*, not by a closing date:
  > "Commence study in Trimester 3, 2025 or any trimester in 2026 or 2027"

  As of 12 October 2026 that window is still open — Trimester 3 2026 has not yet commenced and 2027 trimesters follow. The record's rolling / no-closing-date wording and its null deadline date are accurate.

- **Eligibility — confirmed.** Every distinct claim in the record appears verbatim on the page:
  > "To be eligible you must: - Be a citizen of a country other than Australia or New Zealand. - Have a minimum GPA in previous studies of 4.5 on a 7 point scale or equivalent. - Satisfy all academic and English language entry requirements for your chosen undergraduate or postgraduate coursework program. - Be a full-time student starting in Trimester 3, 2025 or any trimester in 2026 or 2027."

  The page adds restrictions the record does not list — excluded programmes (Bachelor of Medical Science, Bachelor of Dental Health Science, Doctor of Medicine, Master of Dentistry, Master of Speech Pathology, MBA, Graduate Diploma of Flight Management, and more), an ongoing pass-all-courses and full-time-enrolment requirement, and International Fee Paying status. All additive, none contradictory.

- **Funding — confirmed, and correctly *not* called fully funded.**
  > "Award value: 20% of tuition fees"

  and from the terms:
  > "The scholarship covers 20% of undergraduate or postgraduate coursework tuition fees only for the 'Duration' of the program on meeting the ongoing enrolment requirements (see below)."

  This is tuition only — no airfare, stipend, health cover or living allowance. The record's "Tuition Fee Waiver (20% of tuition fees)" is the right characterisation; a tuition-only award is not fully funded, and the record does not claim that it is.

- **Degree levels — confirmed, with one precision note.** The page covers undergraduate and postgraduate coursework, which includes the master's level the record lists, and it rules out doctoral study:
  > "Level of study: Most undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs (some exclusions apply)"

  and from the terms:
  > "Excludes students enrolling in Higher Degree Research, non-award, Study Abroad Program, Diploma programs, Masters Qualifying Program, MBA (program code 5158), Bachelor of Medical Science (1306, 1280, Hons 2099), Bachelor of Dental Health Science (1577, Hons 2126), Master of Dentistry (5703), Doctor of Medicine (5099), Master of Speech Pathology (5706, 5567), Graduate Diploma of Flight Management, English language programs at the Griffith English Language Institute, programs that are only offered part time, and programs delivered onshore or offshore in collaboration with a partner organisation."

  Nothing here contradicts "Undergraduate, Master's" — but the page's scope is wider than that label, because other postgraduate *coursework* qualifications (not just master's degrees) also qualify. The record is accurate but understates the eligible field.

## What a person should know before trusting this record

- **The facts are sound and the URL is live and canonical** — no change to `official_source_url` is needed.
- **This is a partial award, not a full ride.** It covers 20% of tuition fees only. A student reading "scholarship" may reasonably assume more; the card should keep the "Tuition Fee Waiver (20%)" framing, and should not be grouped with fully-funded awards.
- **There is no deadline to miss.** There is no separate application. Assessment happens on the study application, and success is communicated in the Letter of Offer, under "Tuition and other fees – Total program fees". So the honest deadline display is the recurring "no closing date" wording, not a future date.
- **The quoted windows are a snapshot on a clock.** The page still lists Trimester 3 2025 (already past), and the terms state that "Students who defer commencement to 2028 or later are not eligible to receive the scholarship." This record must be re-checked before the 2028 intake, or the eligibility text will silently age out.
- **The card omits the exclusion list**, which matters in practice: a student in medicine, dentistry, speech pathology, the MBA or flight management would find this scholarship does not apply to them at all. Also worth surfacing: "If a student is awarded a Griffith University scholarship that is worth more than 20% of tuition fees, the scholarship at the higher value will apply rather than this scholarship."
- **Suggested reword, optional:** `degree_levels` could be changed from "Undergraduate, Master's" to the provider's own wording, "Most undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs (some exclusions apply)". The current label is not wrong — it is just narrower than reality.
