## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **The round is closed.** The only published cycle shut at 11.59 pm on
  25 August 2026 and the awards page states all general applications are
  "closed until 2027". On 12 October 2026 the record cannot be verified as
  open; re-check around May–July 2027 for the next round (deadlines have
  historically fallen in late August).
- **Two eligibility criteria on the official page are missing from the
  record** and could be added at the next update: applicants must not be
  enrolled in secondary school (unless as an adult student), and the FAQ
  excludes post-graduate study and study outside NZQA-approved New Zealand
  institutions.
- **Award values can change** — the page says values "may differ from year to
  year depending on the funds available".
- **Minor site quirk:** the FAQ page's HTML title reads "Research Awards"
  although its content is the Education Awards FAQ; the content is correct.
- **Do not conflate awards:** the only New Horizons award currently open is
  the Sonja Davies Peace Award (closes 30 October 2026), a different fund.
