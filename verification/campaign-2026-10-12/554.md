## Claim-by-claim

- **Awarding body — confirmed.** "La Secretaría de Ciencia, Humanidades, Tecnología
  e Innovación (Secihti), a través de la Dirección General de Becas y Apoyos a la
  Comunidad Científica y Humanística (DGBACCYH) ... en el marco del Acuerdo de
  cooperación ... entre la Secihti y la Organización de los Estados Americanos,
  firmado el 12 de noviembre de 2025". The OAS selects, publishes results and pays;
  Secihti validates the eligible-candidate list.
- **Deadline — confirmed.** Calendar: "Fecha de cierre del proceso  30 de noviembre
  de 2026" — matches the record exactly and is still in the future. (The calendar
  also shows notification 4–10 September 2026 and formalisation 7–18 September
  2026, i.e. the selection stages have already run; 30 November 2026 is the
  administrative close of the process.)
- **Eligibility — confirmed.** "a) Declarar en su perfil único tener la nacionalidad
  de alguno de los Estados Miembros de la OEA, con excepción de México. b) Haber
  obtenido una Beca Secihti Nacional en el marco de las siguientes convocatorias"
  (the listed 2025 second-period and 2026 first-period postgraduate-scholarship
  calls). Selection criterion 1: "Ser persona becaria nacional admitida e inscrita
  en áreas de Física, Matemáticas y Ciencias de la Tierra, Biología y Química,
  Ingenierías, Medicina y Ciencias de la Salud (a excepción de la formación de
  especialidades y subespecialidades médicas) Biotecnología y Ciencias Agropecuarias."
  Exclusion: previous recipients of an OEA complementary support, and anyone with a
  default record under programme S190.
- **Funding — confirmed as a stipend, and it is not fully funded as a standalone
  award.** "Por parte de la OEA — Un aporte único con valor de $1,200 USD (Mil
  doscientos dólares estadounidenses), pagados en pesos mexicanos ... para gastos
  de instalación"; "Por parte de la Secihti — 1. Apoyo económico mensual, de acuerdo
  con el Tabulador de Becas Nacionales 2026. 2. Servicio médico proporcionado por
  el Instituto de Seguridad y Servicios Sociales de los Trabajadores del Estado
  (ISSSTE)." It is a complement bolted onto an existing Secihti national
  scholarship.
- **Degree levels — confirmed.** "Ser persona becaria nacional vigente en un programa
  de posgrado escolarizado", with criterion 8 "Nivel de estudio (doctorado sobre
  maestría)" — master's and doctorate both covered.

## Before you trust this record

- This is an **indirect-access** call: there is no open application form. Secihti
  screens its own current national-scholarship holders and sends the eligible list
  to the OAS, which selects and disburses. Although the call is officially
  "Abierta" and its process close (30 Nov 2026) is in the future, the selection and
  formalisation windows already ran in September 2026, so a brand-new applicant
  today cannot realistically join this cycle.
- The published award is a **top-up** (monthly stipend per the 2026 Tabulador +
  ISSSTE medical cover + one-off USD 1,200 installation grant), not a full
  scholarship — useful context if the catalogue card could be read as "fully funded".
- Re-check after 30 November 2026 (status should move to results/closed) and watch
  for a 2027 edition under the same Secihti–OAS cooperation agreement. Contacts in
  the document: asignacionbn@secihti.mx and scholarships@oas.org.
- Caveat on quotes: they were transcribed from a pypdf text extraction of the PDF;
  spacing artefacts in the extraction were normalised, no wording was changed.
