## What a person should know before trusting the record

- There is **no single ADB deadline**. Applicants apply to their chosen academic
  institution (a "Designated Institution"), which sets its own intake deadline;
  documents should reach the institution at least six (6) months before the
  intake. To answer "when is this due?" you must check the specific institution
  and intake — that question cannot be answered from ADB's pages alone.
- "Fully Funded" is accurate for the core award, but the official
  Procedures-for-Applying page lists material **exclusions**: it "does not cover
  expenses for the scholar's family; extra-curricular courses or training;
  additional travel during the course of the study program; additional expenses
  related to supplementary educational materials, field trips, or participation
  in workshops, seminars, or internships… or purchase of educational equipment
  such as computers."
- Minor, non-material note: the main page says "about 130 graduate scholarships
  a year" while the Procedures page header says "about 135" — not relevant to any
  record field.
