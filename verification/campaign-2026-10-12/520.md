## Verdict in one line

The official page was read and it confirms the awarding body, the eligibility rules, the funding
package and the degree level — but the record carries **no deadline for the cycle that is open
today**, and the deadline text it does carry comes from an AfDB table last updated in 2022 that two
of the four partner universities now contradict. So the record cannot be moved to `active`.

## The quotes that carry the verdict

**Awarding body — confirmed.**

> "The Japan Africa Dream Scholarship (JADS) is a capacity building program by the African
> Development Bank and the Government of Japan."

Repeated almost word for word by APU, Hiroshima and the University of Tokyo on their own pages.

**Degree levels — confirmed.**

> "The program offers high-achieving African students a two-year scholarship opportunity to pursue
> post-graduate studies at master's level in energy, agriculture, health, environmental
> sustainability, and engineering."

Sophia accepts a "Master program", Hiroshima lists Master's Programmes, APU lists master's
programmes and an MBA. No doctoral level anywhere.

**Eligibility — confirmed, every claim.**

> "Candidates should be 35 years old or younger, and in good health."
>
> "Applicants should have at least 1 year of paid employment in their home country or in other
> African countries after receiving their Bachelors (or equivalent university) degree within the
> past 3 years."
>
> "Must be a citizen of one of the African Development Bank's regional member countries."
>
> "Candidates should gain admission to an approved master's degree course at a Japanese or African
> partner university – application for admission to be done directly through the partner
> universities (Refer to list of universities and approved courses)."

APU's page repeats "Be 35 years old or less" and the one-year employment rule independently.

**Funding — confirmed as partial, not full.**

> "The JADS Scholarship provides the recipient with the following benefits: Economy class air
> travel between a recipient's home country and the host university at the start of the study
> program and immediately following the end of the scholarship period. Tuition for a graduate
> program and the cost of basic medical insurance, usually obtained through the university. A
> monthly subsistence allowance to cover living expenses, including books. … Please note that all
> JADS scholarships cover the duration of the graduate program or two years at most."

And explicitly:

> "The JADS Scholarship does not cover: VISA application(s) costs … Expenses related to
> supplementary educational materials, field trips, participation in workshops/seminars, or
> internships; or Educational equipment such as computers."

So it is more than tuition-only, but it is not an unconditional full ride either — "Partially
Funded" is a fair label, though a reviewer should confirm that is the house convention. APU
itemises the same package at a "Monthly subsistence allowance of 150,000 JPY (including housing)".

**Deadline — this is why the record is not `active`.**

The record's structural claim holds up: there is no central AfDB deadline, and

> "Important: All applications should go through the partner universities, no separate application
> to the African Development Bank is necessary."

But the partner windows the record quotes are from an AfDB page last updated **09-Nov-2022**, and
they no longer match the partners' own pages:

| Partner | Record quotes (AfDB, 2022) | Partner's own live page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| APU | "April Enrollment: October / September Enrollment: April" | "For April 2027 enrollment, please submit your application by September 15, 2026. For September 2027 enrollment, please submit your application by November 5, 2026 or January 8, 2027." |
| Hiroshima | "Month of Intake: April … Application period: Nov 8-19" and an April/October course | "The application deadline for IAAS for October 2027 enrollment is January 29, 2027, at 17:00 (JST)." and "JADS at Hiroshima University is only available for October enrollment." |
| UTokyo | "Schedule A (application period: mid June) Schedule B (application period: mid November)" | Schedule A application period June, Schedule B November — still matches |
| Sophia | "Web entry：Mid of March ~ Early April" | "The web entry period is from middle of March to early April." — still matches |

The record's `deadline_date` is `null`, so a currently-open deadline exists nowhere in it. As of
today the programme **is** running for 2027 entry — APU's September 2027 window closes 5 November
2026 or 8 January 2027, and Hiroshima's October 2027 window closes 29 January 2027 — but those
dates belong to the universities, not to the awarding body, which is why I have recorded them in
`deadline_text` and left `deadline_date` null rather than invent a single central date.

## What a person should know before trusting this record

1. **Trust the body, eligibility, funding and level.** Those four were all read on the awarding
   body's own pages and all agree with the record.
2. **Do not trust the deadline text as written.** It is a faithful quote of the AfDB page, but that
   page is four years old and its APU and Hiroshima windows are wrong for the 2027 cycle. Anyone
   applying from the record's current wording would have the wrong month for APU and would look for
   an April intake at Hiroshima that does not exist.
3. **Applications are partner-driven.** There is no central call and no central date to chase; a
   reader must check the specific partner university. The `deadline_precision: varies` in the
   record is honest.
4. **The AfDB partner list may itself be incomplete.** It still lists only Japanese universities
   and says the list "will be completed with African universities once the Bank finalizes the
   agreement process", yet the Eligibility Criteria page already accepts "a Japanese or African
   partner university". An AfDB press release dated 14-Sep-2026 describes JADS as supporting study
   "at both African and Japanese universities"; I could not read that press release (HTTP 403 on
   afdb.org), so the African expansion is unverified and should be confirmed by a human.
5. **One small defect in the record.** Its `deadline_label` says Kogakuin University is listed with
   "Please refer to the program website." On the AfDB page that sentence sits under Kyoto
   University's approved-course list; Kogakuin appears only with a contact address and a brochure
   PDF link. Not material to the programme, but the quote is misattributed.
6. **Page freshness at check time:** AfDB main page 11-Sep-2024; Eligibility Criteria, Application
   Procedures and List of Universities 09-Nov-2022; Scholarship Benefits 26-Oct-2021; Sophia page
   May 2025. APU, Hiroshima and UTokyo pages carry live 2027 schedules. The next re-check should go
   to the partner universities, not to the AfDB table.
