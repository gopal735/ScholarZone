## The quotes that carry the verdict

**Awarding body — confirmed**

> "The Inter-University Council for East Africa (IUCEA) hereby invites suitably
> qualified nationals of the East African Community (EAC) to apply for Cohort 4
> of the KfW-funded Scholarships to pursue Master's studies in an EAC Partner
> State other than their own."

> "The EAC Scholarship Programme (EACSP) is a regional initiative of the East
> African Community (EAC) and the Federal Republic of Germany. Implemented by
> the Inter-University Council for East Africa…"

**Deadline — confirmed as a closed cycle**

> "Opening date**20 August 2026** Closing date**10 September 2026 · 17:00 EAT**"

**Eligibility — confirmed, all four claims**

> "Be a citizen of any of the 8 EAC Partner States; Be below 35 years of age at
> the time of application; Apply to study in an EAC Partner State other than
> their country of citizenship; Possess a Bachelor's Degree, with a minimum of
> Second Class Upper Division, Distinction or equivalent qualification;"

The two-application rule is also confirmed: "Applicants must complete both stages
of the application process: Apply for admission to an eligible Master's
programme at a participating university, and submit a separate application for
the EAC Scholarship Programme."

**Funding — confirmed as fully funded**

> "Full **tuition fees** payable directly to the host university." · "A monthly
> **stipend of** **EUR 380** to cover accommodation, meals, local transport and
> other living expenses." · "Medical insurance coverage for the duration of the
> scholarship." · "Visa/ Entry fee: Where payment of entry pass into the host
> country or student pass is required."

Also in the package: a return economy air ticket, a EUR 200 arrival allowance,
EUR 650 for study materials, a one-time EUR 1,500 study and research allowance.

**Degree levels — Master's only**

> "Scholarship duration**2 Years** Available awards**Up to 120 Scholarships**"

All 60 listed eligible programmes are Master's degrees (MSc, M Tech, MA, MBA).

## What a person should know before trusting this record

1. **The call is closed.** Applications closed 10 September 2026 at 17:00 EAT.
   Successful students were to be notified in December 2026. The card should not
   be presented as an open opportunity.
2. **The record is otherwise accurate.** Deadline date and time, the eight eligible
   EAC Partner States, the age and degree requirements, the fully funded package
   and the Master's-only level all match IUCEA's page exactly.
3. **Only four of the eight Partner States host awards.** The 60 eligible
   programmes sit at universities in Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda and Tanzania, though
   applicants may be nationals of any of the eight states.
4. **A next cohort is not guaranteed soon.** Phase II started in October 2025 and
   is stated to support "at least 280 students across three cohorts", so Cohort 4
   may be the last of this phase. Re-check
   <https://iucea.org/call-for-applications/> rather than assuming a Cohort 5.
5. **Nothing was fabricated.** This verdict rests on the page text quoted above,
   not on the URL or a search snippet.
