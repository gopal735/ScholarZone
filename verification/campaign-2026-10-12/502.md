## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The funding is a living-cost stipend (€1,500/month + a statutory health-insurance contribution, typically 3.5 years, non-repayable). It is **not** tuition funding and is **not** labelled "Fully Funded" in the record — that is correct and should be preserved.
- There is **no application deadline date**. Applying is rolling; the guidance is "as early as possible", operationally up to about two years before the dissertation is submitted. Do not display a calendar deadline for this record.
- German proficiency matters: B2 is accepted without a certificate, but a proof of German is required even when the dissertation is written in English.
- Medicine is excluded; all other doctoral disciplines in Germany are funded.
