## Findings

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is Dalhousie's own (dal.ca, Faculty of Graduate Studies). "Killam Predoctoral Scholarships are awarded competitively to outstanding doctoral students across all disciplines, including the sciences, engineering, humanities, and social sciences. Supported by the Killam Trusts, these scholarships are intended to advance Canada's future by fostering excellence in graduate education and research."

**Deadline — confirmed, currently open cycle.** Matches the record exactly:

> "Deadlines for non-Tri-agency applicants: Round one – October 16, 2026 at midnight AT; Round two – opens December 1, 2026 – submissions due January 31, 2027 at midnight AT"

Round one (16 October 2026) is four days after today, so the 2026/2027 competition is open. "Award offers will be made to successful candidates via email by May 15, 2027."

**Eligibility — confirmed.** All record claims appear verbatim on the page:

> "This scholarship is open to both Canadian and international students pursuing a full time doctoral (PhD) degree at Dalhousie University."

> "To be successful in receiving a Killam scholarship, applicants must demonstrate exceptional academic achievement, strong research potential, and a clear likelihood of contributing to the advancement of learning in their field. Considerations beyond academic excellence include character, ethical conduct, and contributions to a respectful and inclusive community."

> "This scholarship must be taken up no later than the 7th term in the PhD program. You must have spent less than six terms (24 months) in your PhD as of May 1st of the award year to be eligible to apply."

**Funding — confirmed as a stipend, not fully funded.** "Value: Up to $42,000 for up to three years"; "The base value of this scholarship is $42,000 per year ($14,000 per term) for up to three years, provided the award holder maintains eligibility." Concurrent Tri-agency CGRS-D holders are topped up to $48,000 per year ($16,000 per term), and new scholars relocating to Nova Scotia get a travel reimbursement. No tuition coverage is claimed, consistent with the record's "Stipend" funding type (it should not be presented as "Fully Funded").

**Degree levels — confirmed.** "Degree level: Doctoral (PhD)". The FGS doctoral listing card uses the record's exact title: "Killam Predoctoral Scholarship Level 2 (Doctoral) — Doctoral • Entrance or in-program scholarship • Canadian or international students • $42,000/yr • Up to 3 years".

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The published deadlines apply to **non-Tri-agency applicants**; applicants who have submitted or intend to submit a Tri-agency CGRS-D through Dalhousie in the current cycle are considered automatically for Killam funding, so the deadline shown on the card is the non-Tri-agency deadline.
- The award is a stipend only. The record does not claim "Fully Funded", and that wording should stay off this card.
- The detail page is titled "Killam Predoctoral Scholarship" while the FGS listing uses the full name "Killam Predoctoral Scholarship Level 2 (Doctoral)"; the URL slug "killam-master-doctoral.html" now hosts doctoral content only. Re-check numbering/wording at the next cycle.
