## Adjudication

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is the awarding organisation's own: "The DOE Office of Science's Office of Workforce Development for Teachers and Scientists (WDTS) manages this program for DOE in collaboration with the Oak Ridge Institute for Science and Education (ORISE) and other partners." This matches the record exactly.

**Deadline — confirmed, cycle currently open.** Main page: "The Albert Einstein Distinguished Educator Fellowship Program is now open for applications. Applications are due November 5, 2026 at 8:00pm ET." Key Dates page: "Application Deadline / 8:00pm Eastern November 5, 2026" with "On-line Application Opens August 3, 2026." Today is 12 October 2026, so the November 5, 2026 deadline is in the future and the cycle is open — matching the record's deadline of 5 November 2026 (8:00pm ET) and the 3 August 2026 opening.

**Eligibility — confirmed, all six claims.** The Eligibility page states: "Be a U.S. citizen at the time of applying (by the November 5, 2026 application deadline)."; "Have a minimum of five complete years of full-time classroom teaching experience prior to submitting the application."; "Have been teaching full-time in a public or private elementary or secondary school for at least five of the last seven years in a science, technology, engineering, or mathematics (STEM) discipline."; "Be currently employed full-time in a public or private elementary or secondary school or school district in the U.S."; "Be able to obtain a leave of absence from teaching in order to participate in the program for the full eleven months."; "Be able to provide a letter of recommendation from a school district official as one of their three required letters of recommendation."

**Funding — confirmed as a stipend.** Benefits page: "Receives a monthly living stipend of $8,200." Fellows also get a travel allowance up to $5,000, an education allowance up to $1,000, relocation reimbursement up to $3,500, and a health-insurance stipend up to $550/month (individual) or $1,300/month (family). No tuition element — it is a professional fellowship, not a degree.

**Degree levels — confirmed.** "The Albert Einstein Distinguished Educator Fellowship (AEF) Program provides a unique opportunity for accomplished K-12 educators in the fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) to serve in the national education arena. Fellows spend eleven months working in Federal agencies or in U.S. Congressional offices..." — a non-degree professional fellowship for K-12 STEM educators, exactly as recorded.

## What a reviewer should know

- The official source URL on the record (https://science.osti.gov/wdts/einstein) is live and canonical; no change needed.
- One internal quirk on the official site: the Key Dates page is headed "Key Dates for the 2027-2028 AEF Program Application process." while its orientation line reads "Orientation meeting for the 2026 – 2027 AEF Program Fellows." The deadline itself (November 5, 2026, 8:00pm ET) is stated consistently on both the main page and Key Dates, so this does not affect the verdict.
- The record's deadline_label idiom — "typically opens in mid-August, with applications due in mid-November" — is looser than the published dates (opens August 3, due November 5). It could be replaced with the official wording: "Application Deadline: 8:00pm Eastern, November 5, 2026 (On-line Application opened August 3, 2026)".
- The stipend ($8,200/month) and allowance amounts are set per cycle and may change; the next re-check (expected around August 2027) should re-read the Benefits page rather than assume these figures carry over.
