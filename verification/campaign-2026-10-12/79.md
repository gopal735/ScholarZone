# Verification memo — record 79: Edinburgh Global Research Scholarship

**Verdict: `quarantined` · Confidence: high · Checked: 12 October 2026**

## What was checked

The record's `official_source_url` (https://www.ed.ac.uk/student-funding/postgraduate/international)
is the University of Edinburgh's generic "Funding for international students"
landing page. It was read in full: it does **not** mention the Edinburgh Global
Research Scholarship anywhere, so it cannot support any claim in the record.

No live official page for the programme could be found on ed.ac.uk:

- Plausible scholarship paths (e.g.
  `https://www.ed.ac.uk/student-funding/postgraduate/global-research-scholarship`,
  `https://registryservices.ed.ac.uk/student-funding/postgraduate/global-research-scholarship`)
  return **404**.
- The scholarship site navigation for international postgraduates, the
  "Multi-regional awards" listing, and the institutional Scholarship Search
  contain no trace of it.
- The only official mention anywhere is the University's Research Explorer prize
  record — a **2019** award to a named recipient — not a live programme page.

The University's current flagship PhD scheme for international students is the
**Edinburgh Doctoral College Scholarships** (University tuition fee plus a
UKRI-rate stipend; open to UK **and** overseas students; live 2027-2028 cycle
with per-school deadlines, e.g. Biological Sciences 16 November 2026, Business
School 2 February 2027). This is a different, more generous scheme.

Aggregator pages (globaladmissions.com) still advertise an "Edinburgh Global
Research Scholarship 2026-2027" with a 1 February 2026 deadline — a **past**
cycle — and internally contradict themselves with an "estimated 30 April 2027"
note. Their own "Official Website" links resolve to the generic funding page
that does not mention the scholarship. They are not evidence.

## What a person should know before trusting this record

- **The programme appears to no longer exist as a live offering.** The University
  of Edinburgh publishes nothing about an Edinburgh Global Research Scholarship
  on its live site, and the recorded source URL is the wrong page. Historically
  (e.g. 2018-2019 cycles) the scholarship covered the difference between the
  home and overseas PhD tuition rates for around 30 overseas students per year;
  that role is now filled by the Edinburgh Doctoral College Scholarships, which
  award **tuition plus a stipend** to UK and overseas students alike.
- **Do not rely on aggregator pages** for this record. They keep the listing
  alive with a February deadline that belongs to a past cycle and cannot point
  to any official page.
- **Human decision needed:** retire the record, relabel it as historical, or
  repoint applicants to the Edinburgh Doctoral College Scholarships. No
  confirmation of any currently-open cycle exists, so this must not be marked
  active.
