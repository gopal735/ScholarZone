## Why the verdict is contradicting

**Deadline is the problem.** The record stores `deadline_date: 2027-09-08` with day precision, implying an open cycle running to 8 September 2027. No official page states any 2027 date. The only published cycle is 2026:

> "9 September 2026: deadline to submit proposals (TBC)" — official 2026 call page

> "The call will close on 9 September 2026 and is expected to fund nearly 1600 projects." — official MSCA news item

> "The Postdoctoral Fellowships 2026 call for applications opened on 9 April 2026 and will close on 9 September 2026, 17.00, CEST." — official MSCA "6 steps" page

That deadline passed a month before today (12 October 2026); evaluation results are only expected in February 2027. No 2027 call, opening date or deadline has been published on any official page read, so **no cycle is currently open**. The record's specific 2027-09-08 date has no official basis, which contradicts the record in a material way.

## What did check out (all confirmed from the same official pages)

- **Awarding body:** European Commission / MSCA. "This site is managed by the European Commission, Directorate-General for Education, Youth, Sport and Culture."
- **Eligibility:** "MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships will be open to excellent researchers of any nationality." / "should have a PhD degree at the time of the deadline for applications". The mobility rule matches the record verbatim: "they must not have resided or carried out their main activity (work, studies, etc.) in the country of the beneficiary (for European Postdoctoral Fellowships), or the host organisation for the outgoing phase (for Global Postdoctoral Fellowships) for more than 12 months in the 36 months immediately before the call deadline." The record's reintegration/displaced-researcher claim is confirmed: the scheme "is open to researchers wishing to reintegrate in Europe, to those who are displaced by conflict, as well as to researchers with high potential who are seeking to restart their careers in research."
- **Funding (Fully Funded):** confirmed, not tuition-only. "The EU provides support for the recruited researcher in the form of a living allowance, a mobility allowance, if applicable, family, long-term leave and special needs allowances", plus "funding … for research, training and networking activities" and "management and indirect costs". Indicative budget: EUR 434 million.
- **Degree levels (Postdoctoral):** confirmed. "The Postdoctoral Fellowships action targets researchers holding a PhD who wish to carry out their research activities abroad, acquire new skills and develop their careers."

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The programme exists, is officially run by the European Commission, and is fully funded — the body, eligibility, funding and degree-level fields are accurate.
- Do **not** trust the `deadline_date` of 2027-09-08: nothing official publishes it. The 2026 call closed 9 September 2026 at 17:00 CEST; results are expected February 2027; first projects start May 2027. The next call (expect HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01) historically opens in April and closes in the second week of September, but is **not yet officially announced** as of today.
- The record omits one eligibility condition worth knowing: applicants "must have a maximum of eight years experience in research, from the date of the award of their PhD degree" (career breaks, time outside research, and third-country research by EU/associated-country nationals reintegrating to Europe do not count).
- One minor unresolved detail: a search-engine snippet quoting the EU portal showed the 2026 deadline as "08 September 2026", while the MSCA site's own pages say 9 September 2026. Both dates are past, so the verdict is unaffected.
