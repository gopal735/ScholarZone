## What agrees with the record

**Awarding body.** Confirmed. Aurora is the establishing and administering body, jointly with Cancer Australia:

> "Established by the Aurora Education Foundation (Aurora) and Cancer Australia the First Nations Cancer Scholarship (scholarship) supports Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander graduate students, health professionals, and early-career researchers to undertake full-time postgraduate study or research in a cancer-related field."

**Eligibility.** Both distinct claims confirmed almost verbatim:

> "To be eligible for the First Nations Cancer Scholarship, Indigenous students must be undertaking full-time postgraduate study or research in a cancer-related field in health, research or policy. Applicants must be also able to demonstrate how their study or research will impact Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities in improving cancer-related outcomes."

The portal adds requirements the record omits — descent, community acceptance, and written confirmation of identity from an incorporated Indigenous organisation or Land Council.

**Funding.** "Fully Funded" is supported:

> "Administered by Aurora, each scholarship is valued at up to $120, 000 AUD per year, for up to three years. Recipients will also have access to up to $5,000 AUD per year in tailored academic and wellbeing support."

The award is itemised against fees/HECS, living expenses, travel, equipment and other study costs. It is not tuition-only.

**Degree levels.** The portal's Study Level tags for this scholarship cover the record's list:

> "Study Level: Honours, Graduate Certificate, Graduate; Mid-Career Research; Postdoctoral; Postgraduate Coursework; Postgraduate Research"

## What does not

**Deadline — no currently open cycle.** The date *in the record is right*; the *availability is wrong*. The portal marks the round closed a month ago:

> "Open: 16/06/2026 2026-06-16T00:00:00+00:00; Close: 11/09/2026 2026-09-11T00:00:00+00:00; Closed"

Today is 12 October 2026. That cycle closed on 11 September 2026, and no successor round is published on any Aurora page. Aurora's programme page carries no deadline at all. So the record cannot be shown as an open cycle today.

**Award window conflict between the two official bodies.** Aurora says:

> "Up to four scholarships will be awarded yearly from 2024 to 2026."

Cancer Australia's programme page instead says "Scholarships will be awarded each year from 2024 – 2027." **I could not read that page** — every fetch of `canceraustralia.gov.au` failed with a transport error, so that wording comes from a search-index snippet, not from the page. Treat it as unverified until someone can load it.

**Degree-level wording.** The eligibility text is narrower than the portal's filter tags — "a postgraduate course (graduate certificate, masters or PhD) … OR … written confirmation from a recognised research institute". "Honours" and "Postdoctoral" are portal tags rather than stated eligible levels, and "Postgraduate Certificate" looks like a mislabel for "graduate certificate", which the record already lists.

## Before trusting the record

1. **The opportunity is closed as of today.** Any card quoting a live deadline here is misleading; the 2026 round shut on 11 September 2026.
2. **Whether a 2027 round exists is genuinely undecided.** Aurora says the award runs 2024–2026; Cancer Australia says 2024–2027 and its site was unreachable from here. Someone with access should load the Cancer Australia page and the Indigenous Pathways Portal around mid-2027.
3. **"Fully Funded" carries a hedge.** Aurora calls the award "a contribution towards the cost of" study and excludes expenses the university should bear, even though $120k/yr covers fees and living costs broadly. Confirm how the catalogue defines that label.
4. **The eligibility summary is sound but incomplete** — it drops the documented-identity requirements, which are the practical gate for applicants.
