- **Deadline (open cycle):** "The Office of Science Graduate Student Research (SCGSR) program is now open for applications. **Applications are due November 4, 2026, at 5:00PM ET.**"
- **Deadline mechanics:** "At the submission deadline (shown in red), the online application system will close after which no additional materials will be accepted. **The online application system closes at 5:00 PM Eastern Time, so please plan accordingly.**"
- **Awarding body:** "The SCGSR program is sponsored and managed by the DOE Office of Science's Office of Workforce Development for Teachers and Scientists (WDTS), in collaboration with the six Office of Science research programs, the Isotope R&D and production program, and the DOE National Laboratories and User Facilities. Online application and awards administration support is provided by Oak Ridge Institute of Science and Education (ORISE) under Oak Ridge Associated Universities (ORAU)."
- **Eligibility:** "Applicants must be U.S. Citizens or Lawful Permanent Residents (LPR) at the time of application and be at least 18 years of age at the time of applying." … "Applicants must be enrolled full-time in a Qualified Graduate Program with the Ph.D. as their degree objective. At the time of application, applicants must have obtained the Ph.D. candidacy at their home institution, and must have established a defined graduate thesis project and graduate thesis advisor."
- **Collaborator requirement:** "The applicant and their primary graduate thesis advisor are responsible for identifying a collaborating research scientist at a DOE laboratory and jointly developing the research proposal as part of the SCGSR application process."
- **Funding:** "The SCGSR program provides supplemental awards to support part of a graduate student's thesis research to be conducted at a DOE laboratory/facility. Supplemental awards are intended to cover incremental costs associated with living and travel expenses during the award period." … "Awardees are eligible to receive a monthly stipend in the amount of up to $3,600 for general living expenses" … "limited to a total travel benefit of up to $3,000 per award."

## Before you trust the record

- The deadline is real but short-lived: it passes on **4 November 2026**. After that date the card must not present it as open; the next cycle (2027 Solicitation 1) is tentative only — opens February 2027, due May 2027.
- Funding is modest and specific: a living stipend (up to $3,600/month) plus travel reimbursement (up to $3,000 per award). No tuition, no stipend guarantee beyond the "under normal circumstances" note on the Benefits page. Keep the "Research Funding" label; never let it render as fully funded.
- Age/citizenship nuance: LPR holders must separately obtain host-laboratory access approval, which each lab sets — worth knowing when reading the eligibility summary.
