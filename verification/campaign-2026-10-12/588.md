## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **Round one closes 16 October 2026 at midnight AT** — four days after this check. Anyone reading this record after that date is looking at a closed round; round two then runs from 1 December 2026 to 31 January 2027. The deadline line rolls over annually, so re-check it before each cycle.
- **Two routes in, one application only.** A Tri-agency CGRS-D application submitted through Dalhousie in the current cycle is automatically considered for DRES; non-Tri-agency applicants apply through the FGS Scholarship Management Platform selecting "Major Scholarship Competitions 2026/2027". The page warns: "Please do not submit multiple applications, only one application per candidate will be reviewed and all others will be disqualified."
- **$7,000 of the $35,000 is not guaranteed by the institution** — it comes from the supervisor or program, and the exact split is only communicated after acceptance. The award is also conditional on applying to CGRS-D within the first two PhD years, and DRES becomes honorary if CGRS-D ($40,000/yr) or Killam ($42,000/yr, topped up) is held concurrently.
- Award holders owe an annual report to fgs.slo@dal.ca by 31 July each year the award is held.
- Students intending to transfer from a master's to a PhD must apply for a doctoral scholarship *before* officially transferring, otherwise the transfer-date rule affects their funding eligibility window.
