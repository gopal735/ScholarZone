## What happened to this programme

The NFP was folded into the Orange Knowledge Programme (OKP), itself run 2018-2024 by Nuffic, and the OKP ended in 2024. The Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairs' own evaluation of the OKP (published December 2025) describes OKP as "a successor to the Netherlands Fellowship Programme (NFP) and the Netherlands Initiative for Capacity Building in Higher Education (NICHE)" — I only have the search-indexed excerpt of that PDF, not the document itself, so it is not counted as a read source here; the two Nuffic-run pages above are sufficient on their own.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **Do not read this record as an open scholarship.** The programme no longer runs: its page is gone, and the successor that absorbed it ended in 2024. Nothing in this check found a route to apply.
- The title "(NFP/OTS)" conflates two different programmes. The Orange Tulip Scholarship (OTS) is a separate award that Nuffic also lists as previously managed centrally; it is now administered decentralised by individual Dutch universities. They are not the same programme and should not share one record.
