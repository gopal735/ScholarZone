## Before you trust the record

Nothing in the record needs changing. Two things worth knowing for applicants and for the next re-check:

1. **No separate application exists** — "There is no separate scholarship application required. Students are invited to apply for the Curtin course... All qualifying applications to Curtin are automatically assessed." The 1 Oct 2027 date is the application-window close, not a standalone scholarship deadline.
2. **The excluded-courses list "may change without notice"**, and the listed exclusions are extensive (most health-professions master's and several science degrees). Scholarship continuation also requires maintaining a CWA of 70% from the second study period onwards.
