## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. **Timing nuance.** On the check date (12 October 2026) no application window is literally open: the 2027–28 competition closed on 1 October 2026, and the next window opens 1 July 2027. The record is nonetheless forward-accurate — the Center's own page currently publishes 1 October 2027 as the deadline for the 2028–29 cycle, exactly the deadline the record carries. The competition runs on a fixed annual calendar (opens July 1, closes October 1, letters due October 8), so the record's deadline_label without a year is correct.
2. **Five-year rule has two exceptions.** The page states the five-year-since-terminal-degree rule "or the applicant meets the requirements for one of two unique fellowships: 1) Geiss-Hsu Fellowship … 2) Philip L. Quinn Fellowship, for a female-identified early-career philosopher." So "at least five years before applying" is not absolute; the record's eligibility wording is still accurate as the general rule.
3. **Never describe this as "fully funded."** It is a half-salary replacement stipend plus travel for the Fellow and family; housing, research travel and living expenses are the Fellow's own responsibility, and researchers must be in residence in North Carolina for the full term.
4. **Next re-check:** the page rolls to the next cycle around July 2027, when the 2029–30 dates replace the 2028–29 dates now published.
