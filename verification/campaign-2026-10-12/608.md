## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. **There is no deadline to miss, and none to trust either.** AUW publishes no closing date;
   admission runs on three intakes per year. The record's "no closing date published; admissions
   open" wording is accurate. If a future re-check finds a published intake deadline, record it and
   upgrade `deadline_precision`.
2. **"Fully Funded" is honest here** — a full scholarship covers tuition, room and board, course
   supplies, student activities and primary healthcare. Partial awards are need-based; nothing is
   guaranteed by admission alone. Aid is decided by the Financial Aid Committee on assessed family
   circumstances (Self-Assessment Form SAF-Bangladesh / SAF-International plus bank statements, tax
   returns, salary certificates).
3. **Two eligibility gaps in the record:** the live page also requires a minimum IB score of 24/45
   for IB applicants, and aid recipients must maintain a CGPA of 2.5. Neither contradicts the
   record but both are worth adding at the next refresh.
4. **Exclusions:** transfer students are ineligible for financial aid. The nondiscrimination
   statement scopes admission to "applicants to the undergraduate program who identify as women".
5. Aid "student cannot apply for any increase in the financial aid after she has started at AUW
   unless a major event occurred that severely changed her family's financial situation" — rare.
