## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. **The deadline is correct and the cycle is genuinely open.** 31 October 2026 is the scholarship deadline for the postgraduate routes; an applicant reading the record on 12 October 2026 can still apply. The undergraduate deadline had already passed (30 September 2026) — consistent with the record's "(2027 intake, postgraduate)" title.
2. **"Fully Funded" is well supported** and is not a tuition-only award: tuition and international student fees, accommodation, meals, medical cover, a living stipend, a book allowance, visa costs, a laptop, an internship opportunity and an air ticket home are all listed. Note it is conditional — "renewable annually subject to satisfactory academic performance and personal conduct, and to participate in the development programmes".
3. **The FAQ page on UCT's own site is dangerously stale.** It says only "Masters (by Research only)" are available, that no applications for "Undergraduates, Honours, Masters by Coursework and Dissertation, Postgraduate Diploma', MBA and PhD will be accepted", and repeats a "30 April 2024" deadline. None of that matches the live apply page, which funds taught postgraduate programmes and closes 31 October 2026. A future re-check must read the apply page, not the FAQ. Someone at UCT should be asked to update or remove the FAQ.
4. **Degree levels in the record are incomplete, not wrong.** The programme also funds an eligible Faculty of Health Sciences postgraduate diploma and honours degrees, and it explicitly excludes part-time study and research-only qualifications. If the catalogue's degree-level lists are meant to be exhaustive, this field should be widened.
5. **A small wording difference worth knowing about:** the "About the Program" page describes scholars as "academically talented young leaders from Sub-Saharan Africa", while the apply page's eligibility list says "Be a citizen of an African country, including South Africa". The apply page is the operative eligibility statement and supports the record's wording, but a strict reader could see the two pages as describing slightly different geographic scopes (Sub-Saharan only vs. all African countries).
6. Applicants must first apply to UCT for academic study and placement before the scholarship application can be submitted; only applicants who receive an offer of admission are eligible for the programme.

## Bottom line

No contradictions found. Awarding body, deadline, eligibility, funding and degree levels all agree with the awarding body's own page, the deadline falls inside an open cycle, and the funding is full-cost. Record 677 can be moved to `active`.
