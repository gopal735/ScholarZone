Each is applied directly to the student account; only successful recipients are notified by email.

**Degree levels** — undergraduate and postgraduate taught, as the record states:

> "This scholarship is available to Non-EU applicants who have received an offer to study on a Masters or Bachelor programme in the Faculty of Engineering and Computing."

No PhD/research programmes are listed under these scholarships, consistent with the record's "Undergraduate, Postgraduate Taught".

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- The awards are **partial fee reductions (EUR 1,850-6,100)**, not full tuition waivers and not fully funded; there is no living-cost, travel or stipend component. The record does not claim otherwise.
- There is **no scholarship deadline**. The 2027-07-01 date is the DCU international undergraduate admissions closing date for the 2027-28 cycle; postgraduate applicants must check programme-level deadlines on the DCU Registry admissions pages.
- Scholarships are **programme-specific**: only the eligible programme codes listed per faculty for 2027-28 qualify, and a recipient of one DCU International scholarship cannot also be considered for another DCU scholarship (e.g. the Merit Scholarship).
- Amounts and programme lists change annually. When DCU rolls the page to the 2028-29 cycle, the URL year suffix and every amount and code must be re-checked.
