## Adjudication

### Awarding body — confirmed
The page is on `campusfrance.org`, the implementing organisation's own domain, and names the funders and the implementer exactly as the record does:

> "This tenth call for applications, funded by the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs (MEAE), in partnership with the Ministry of Higher Education, Research and Space (MESRE), and implemented by Campus France, will enable young international researchers to carry out a research project in France, thereby fostering collaboration with leading French research institutions."

It also states that "The fellowship is **paid directly by Campus France**" and that successful applicants "are granted the status of **French Government Scholarship holders (BGF)**".

### Deadline — confirmed, cycle currently open
The page's TIMELINE section:

> "15 September 2026: Applications open / 15 December 2026: Application deadline / May–June 2027: Notification of results / Between September and December 2027: Successful applicants arrive in France and begin their research stay at French host institutions"

Today is 12 October 2026. Applications opened on 15 September 2026 and the deadline (15 December 2026) is in the future, so the record's "Application deadline: 15 December 2026" / `2026-12-15` is the deadline for the cycle that is currently open, stated exactly. This is not a stale or past deadline.

### Eligibility — confirmed
The page's ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS list:

> "Applicants must: Hold a **doctoral degree awarded less than five years before the application deadline**, i.e. have defended their PhD thesis between December 2021 and December 2026 inclusive; Be **exclusively of foreign nationality** (all nationalities are eligible except French nationality); Apply for the purpose of **undertaking international mobility to France**; Not be residing and professionally active in France at the time of application; Not have previously received a MOPGA fellowship."

This confirms each distinct claim in the record's summary: foreign researchers; PhD defended between December 2021 and December 2026 inclusive; applying from outside France for a research stay in France. The last two bullets are requirements the record's summary does not mention — they are additions, not contradictions. The FAQ also notes a maternity-leave exception that "may be extended by 18 months per child".

### Funding — confirmed as a stipend (not fully funded)
> "Fellowships are awarded for a period of **12 months**."
>
> "Each fellowship includes, exclusively: A **monthly allowance of €2,500**; A **€500 settling-in allowance**; **Health coverage**: health insurance, civil liability insurance and repatriation assistance, followed by supplementary health insurance once the fellow has been registered with the French social security system."

And from the FAQ:

> "Is travel to France covered? No. International travel costs are not covered by the programme. Your host laboratory may, however, be able to provide support with these costs."

The page also states: "Host laboratories do not receive any funding under the programme; the fellowship is intended solely to cover the invited researcher's living expenses." This matches the record's `Stipend` classification. It is a living-expense stipend, so the record must **not** be described as "fully funded": travel is not covered and no tuition or laboratory costs are funded. There is no tuition element because this is a visiting research stay rather than an enrolment.

### Degree levels — confirmed (Postdoc)
> "MOPGA has become a flagship programme for **postdoctoral mobility** in France and one of the leading fellowship programmes of the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs (MEAE)."

> "Are there MOPGA fellowships for Master's or PhD students? No. There is no MOPGA call for applications for Master's or PhD students."

The page is tagged "Post doc" and requires a doctoral degree defended within the last five years, so the record's "Postdoc" level is correct.

## What a reviewer should know before trusting the record

- **The recorded deadline is live, not stale.** Applications opened 15 September 2026 and close 15 December 2026. Anyone reading this before mid-December 2026 can still apply; after 15 December 2026 the record needs a fresh check.
- **The official source URL on the record is unchanged and canonical.** No new URL is required.
- **Do not market this as fully funded.** It is €2,500/month for 12 months plus a €500 settling-in allowance and health cover. International travel to France is explicitly excluded, and the host laboratory gets nothing.
- **Applications are online only**, through `https://campusfrance.smapply.io/prog/mopga_2027/`; the page states "Applications sent by email will not be considered." Questions go to mopga@campusfrance.org.
- **Two eligibility conditions are missing from the record's summary** and are worth adding: applicants must not have previously received a MOPGA fellowship, and must not be residing or professionally active in France at the time of application. French nationals are excluded; a maternity-leave exception can extend the PhD-defence window by 18 months per child.
- **Other conditions:** the fellowship lasts 12 months and is not renewable, and cannot be combined with a French employment contract (research or postdoctoral) or any other individual funding from a French government entity. Projects must fall within five MOPGA research themes (Earth system sciences; climate change and sustainability sciences; energy transition; societal challenges related to environmental issues; One Health — human, animal and environmental health); humanities and social sciences projects are eligible if they fit the themes.
- **Next re-check (after 15 December 2026):** re-read the Campus France page rather than assuming the allowance amounts or the defence window carry over to the next edition.
