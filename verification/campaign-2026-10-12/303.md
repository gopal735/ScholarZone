## Findings

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is the awarding organisation's own:
> "Camões – Instituto da Cooperação e da Língua, I.P. has in place a set of scholarship programmes within the framework of Portuguese Language and Culture"

**Deadline — contradicted.** The page quotes exactly the deadline the record carries (5–18 May 2026), but that cycle is closed: provisional and definitive result lists for every programme were published in June 2026, and the notice states approved scholarships could only begin from September 2026. On 12 October 2026 the official page shows **no open application cycle** and no announcement of a 2027 cycle.
> "Within the framework of the Research Scholarship Programme, applications are open from 5 to 18 May 2026 (until 5 p.m., Lisbon time)."
> "Applications may be submitted between 5 and 18 May 2026 (until 17:00, Lisbon time)."

**Eligibility — confirmed.** All distinct claims match:
> "This programme is for teachers and researchers, both foreigners and Portuguese living abroad, who wish to undertake specialisation studies in the field of Portuguese language and culture in Portugal, namely master and PhD study programmes at Portuguese universities."

**Funding — contradicted (the material issue).** The record says "Fully Funded". The awarding body's own notices describe a monthly allowance, with tuition, travel and accommodation explicitly the holder's own cost:

> "Research Program – Monthly allowance: €990/month (maximum 11 monthly payments per year)"

> "Travel and accommodation costs are the responsibility of the scholarship holder. The scholarship holder is also responsible for tuition fees and registration at the higher education institution attended."

> "In-person Summer Scholarships — Monthly stipend – €1,150.00" / "Online Summer Scholarships — Covers course registration/tuition fee"

**Degree levels — confirmed** ("Undergraduate (final year), Master's, PhD"):
> "Research Program – Intended for foreign professors and researchers, as well as Portuguese nationals residing abroad, who wish to carry out in Portugal advanced studies in Portuguese language and culture, namely Master's and PhD Programs at Portuguese universities."

The notice fixes durations of two years for Master's and three years for PhD programmes; Fernão Mendes Pinto covers students "attending their final year of undergraduate studies".

## Before trusting this record, a person should know

1. **It is not currently applicable.** The only documented cycle (2026) closed on 18 May 2026 and its results were published in June 2026. No cycle is open on 12 October 2026 and no 2027 call has been announced. Camões, I.P. runs one annual spring window — expect the next around spring 2027 and re-check then.
2. **"Fully Funded" is wrong.** These are monthly living stipends (Research: €990/month, max 11 payments/year; in-person Summer Course: €1,150/month). Tuition, travel and accommodation are the scholarship holder's responsibility; only the online Summer Course variant covers the registration/tuition fee. Even a tuition-only award would not be fully funded — this is less than that.
3. **The recorded URL is dead.** The live pages live under `https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/en/activity-camoes/what-we-do/scholarships/scholarships-camoes-i-p/pt-language-culture-scholarships`.
4. **The record bundles a programme family** (Summer Course, Annual Course, Fernão Mendes Pinto, Research, Pessoa, Vieira) whose amounts and coverage differ per programme; a single "Fully Funded" label is not right for the set.
