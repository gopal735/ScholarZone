The record's funding wording tracks the 2027 guidelines almost literally, and
the official embassy pages add that MEXT also pays tuition and airfare
(Embassy of Japan in Ireland: "MEXT will cover entrance examination,
matriculation and university tuition fees, and flights to and from Japan, and
provide a monthly allowance of between JPY143,000 and JPY145,000 (subject to
change).") — so this is a full award, not a tuition-only one.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- **The cycle is over.** Recruitment for April/September–October 2027 arrival
  happened in April–May 2026, with first screening May–July 2026. Every embassy
  page checked shows the 2027 window closed (Hong Kong explicitly: "Currently
  Open for Application NO"; Pakistan: last date 26 May 2026). MEXT final results
  for this cycle are expected January–March 2027. The record is accurate but
  describes a window that has passed.
- **Deadline genuinely varies by country.** There is no single date to quote;
  the next cycle (2028 arrival) guidelines are expected on the Study in Japan
  site around spring 2027, and applicants must watch their own embassy.
