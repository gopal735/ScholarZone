# GKS (Global Korea Scholarship) Program — Undergraduate Degree Programs — record 516

**Verdict: active (high confidence).** Checked 2026-10-12 on the official Study in Korea / NIIED page.

## What the official page says

- **Awarding body**: National Institute for International Education (NIIED), confirmed in the page footer.
- **Cycle**: "Announcement — NIIED — September", "Application Submission — Embassy Track: September / University Track: September - November", "Screening and Selection — October - December", "Final Results — December".
- **Eligibility**: non-Korean citizenship for the applicant and both parents; under 25 as of the applicable year; the published educational qualification for the track; and academic performance at 80% cumulative / top 20% / the CGPA threshold. All four agree with the record.
- **Funding**: "Airfare, Korean language training fees, tuition, and monthly allowances."
- **Levels**: associate (2-3 years) and bachelor's (4-6 years) after one year of Korean language training.
