## Findings

- **Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is on ACIAR's own domain and describes the programme ACIAR runs through the Australia Awards system.
  > "The John Allwright Fellowship (JAF) is a competitive, merit-based program available to scientists or economists from partner countries currently or recently involved in ACIAR research projects. It offers the opportunity to obtain formal postgraduate qualifications at an Australian tertiary institution."
- **Deadline — cannot confirm as open.** The page banner reads `Applications closed`, and: > "Applications for the 2027 intake are now closed."

  The factsheet gives the recurring window: > "Applications open 1 February – 30 April". The record's 30 April 2026 date matches the 2027 intake's close, but that round has passed. On 12 October 2026 no cycle is open (next intake expected ~1 February 2027).
- **Eligibility — confirmed.** Every distinct claim in the record appears on the page, e.g. > "You are a scientist or economist who is actively involved in an ACIAR research project or small research activity within the last 24 months." The page adds requirements the record does not list (no Australian citizenship/PR, employer and project-leader endorsements, permanent/long-term contract, bachelor-equivalent qualifications, recent English test) — additive, not contradictory.
- **Funding — confirmed (genuinely fully funded).** > "The following benefits generally apply: * Full tuition fees * Return air travel … * Establishment allowance … * Contribution to Living Expenses (CLE) – a fortnightly contribution to basic living expenses …" plus Introductory Academic Program, OSHC, possible pre-course English fees, supplementary academic support and fieldwork airfares. Not a tuition-only award.
- **Degree levels — confirmed.** > "an opportunity to obtain formal postgraduate qualifications, such as a Master by Research or PhD degree at an Australian tertiary institution" — with the caveat "Masters by coursework is not eligible under this scholarship." (Record's "Master's, PhD" is accurate; the master's is research-only.)
