## What a person should know before trusting the record

1. **This is the second-semester cycle.** The Chula LINK Scholarship 2026 runs in
   more than one cycle. The university's 17 April 2026 news release describes the
   *first* semester, whose application window closed on 24 May 2026, and the OIA
   site has a separate first-semester listing at
   `inter.chula.ac.th/funding-opportunity/37857/`. The record (id 652) points at
   `40290`, which is the *second semester, academic year 2026* cycle — the one
   open today. Do not let the two be conflated: the first-semester record's
   deadline is long past, while this one is live until 31 October 2026.

2. **Re-check again in late October 2026.** The deadline is close enough that the
   status flips from `Open` to closed within the month. After 31 October 2026
   this record is no longer verifiable as an open cycle and will need to be moved
   to `outdated` rather than left `active`.

3. **One field wording caveat on funding.** The OIA page's own taxonomy sidebar
   labels the field "Funding Type: Scholarship" and never prints the words
   "fully funded" on the page itself. I treated `Fully Funded` as confirmed
   because the page lists tuition, a monthly living allowance and round-trip
   airfare, which is the substantive meaning. If the catalogue insists on
   provider wording rather than provider substance, that field is the one to
   reconsider.

4. **Faculty-specific extra conditions are not in the record, and that is
   correct.** The Petroleum and Petrochemical College's procedure PDF adds
   conditions for *its own* applicants — minimum GPA 2.75 for master's; 2.75 at
   bachelor's and 3.25 at master's for doctoral; TOEFL (ITP) 500 / CBT 173 /
   iBT 60 or IELTS 5.5; age under 40; a medical certificate; two letters of
   recommendation. None of these appear on the record's page or in its
   eligibility summary, so I have not treated them as a contradiction. They are
   recorded here only so a human knows they exist for that faculty's applicants.
