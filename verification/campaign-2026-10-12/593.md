## What the pages confirm

**Awarding body** — Both pages sit on perimeterinstitute.ca (footer: "Copyright © 2000-2026 Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics"), Waterloo, Ontario. This is the awarding organisation's own site. Confirmed.

**Partner universities and eligibility** — the page lists exactly the seven universities named in the record:

> "These partner universities include:
> - Dalhousie University
> - McMaster University
> - University of Guelph
> - University of Toronto
> - University of Waterloo
> - Western University
> - York University"

and requires a Perimeter-affiliated supervisor plus a separate Expression of Interest:

> "Resident PhD students are supervised by a Faculty, Associate Faculty, or Senior Researcher located at Perimeter Institute but receive their degree from a partnering university where their supervisor has a full or adjunct appointment."

> "Interested students must submit an application for the Perimeter PhD Residency Award in addition to a separate PhD application submitted to one of the partner universities listed above."

The award posting adds: "This is NOT an application to a PhD program. This is a Residency Award that requires: Acceptance to a university PhD program where a Perimeter faculty member is affiliated; and Acceptance by a Perimeter faculty member to work in their group; and Approval by the awards committee".

**Deadline** — No fixed date is published, matching the record's `recurring` precision with no date. The How to Apply page states:

> "NOTE: The Expression of Interest deadline is based on the application deadline of the university you are applying to. Your Expression of Interest must be submitted within one week of submitting your university application (for multiple university applications, use the earliest application date)."

The 2027 cycle posting is currently open (posted 2026/08/13, "Appl Deadline: none"), and the programme page's timeline — "November-January - Students submit PhD application to partner university and Expression of Interest to Perimeter Institute" for a Fall start — shows the cycle for 2027 entry is the one in play today, 12 October 2026. No past deadline is shown anywhere.

**Funding** — the record's "university salary plus Perimeter top-up" matches the page:

> "Resident PhD students at Perimeter are financially supported through their home university (typically for 4 years / 12 academic terms) and are eligible for the following benefits of the PhD Residency Program Award."

> "Top-up funding to $30,000 per year (after fees and tuition) until Term 12. This is based on a September – August academic year and builds on the university department's standard funding package."

The award also carries non-cash benefits (dedicated desk, facilities access, technology grant of up to $2000, mentorship). Note for readers: the $30,000 is the **top-up** to reach a total, not the total package itself.

**Degree levels** — PhD only. The pages describe PhD students and a "PhD Residency Program Award" throughout.

## What a person should know before trusting the record

- Everything the record claims was re-confirmed on the awarding body's own live pages on 2026-10-12. No claim was contradicted.
- The record's empty deadline fields are correct, not missing data: this award genuinely has no published deadline of its own — it inherits the partner university's deadline. Applicants who wait for a "scholarship deadline" will miss the cycle.
- One minor wording tension exists between two official pages: the How to Apply page says the Expression of Interest is due within one week of the university application, while the AcademicJobsOnline posting says it "must be submitted to Perimeter at the same time as an application to a partner university." Applicants should treat the one-week rule as the binding instruction.
