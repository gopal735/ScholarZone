## What the record claims, and what the page says

**Awarding body — confirmed.** The page is DAAD's own site (www2.daad.de) and its heading is
_"Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS) • DAAD."_ It states: _"With its development-oriented postgraduate study programmes, the DAAD promotes the training of specialists from development and newly industrialised countries."_ The awarder is DAAD — the German Academic Exchange Service — as the record says. (The BMZ-funding sub-detail is consistent but not spelled out verbatim on this entry page.)

**Deadline — confirmed as "varies by course", with a live 2027/2028 cycle.** The entry's only deadline statement is
_"Application deadline — Depending on chosen study programme; please check scholarship brochure or the website of your chosen study programme."_
The entry links to the "List of all Postgraduate courses with application deadlines **2027/2028**", so the currently open cycle is the winter-semester 2027/2028 intake. Example from a linked course: the Freiburg MSc Environmental Governance EPOS application window is "15 June to 15 October 2026", i.e. still open today.

**Eligibility — every claim confirmed.**
- _"Graduates with at least two years' professional experience from the following countries"_ (eligible-country / DAC list is linked).
- Bar/"fully": _"Applicants who have been resident in Germany for longer than 15 months at the application deadline cannot be considered."_
- Above-average results: _"far above average results (upper third)"_ (and in Selection: _"top performance third"_).
- Development motivation: _"Candidates can prove their motivation is development-related."_
So: development + newly-industrialised/DAC countries, ≥2 years' work experience, above-average results, development motivation, 15-month Germany exclusion — all confirmed.

**Funding — confirmed fully funded.** _"Depending on academic level, monthly payments of 992 euros for graduates or 1,300 euros (1,400 euros beginning with February 2026) for doctoral candidates — Payments towards health, accident and personal liability insurance cover — Travel allowance ..."_ plus a possible monthly rent subsidy and family allowance. This is a living-cost package, not tuition-only.

**Degree levels — confirmed Master's, PhD.** _"... the chance to take a postgraduate or Master's degree at a state or state-recognised German university, and in exceptional cases to take a doctoral degree, and to obtain a university qualification (Master's/PhD) in Germany."_ Duration: _"12 to 42 months (dependent on study programme)."_

## Before you trust the record

- Safe to mark `active`. All five categories agree with the awarding body's live page, and a 2027/2028 cycle is open today.
- **PhD is the exception, not the rule** — funded "in exceptional cases". "Master's, PhD" is accurate, but Master's is the normal level.
- **Deadline has no single date.** The catalog's parenthetical _"typically August-November annually for next intake"_ is a reasonable generalisation but is *not* verbatim on the page; the page only defers to the chosen programme. Next re-check: refresh per-course deadlines from the links.
