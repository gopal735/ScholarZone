Scholarships for Current Students
The scholarships shown as below are only for the
current international degree students
who were applying for admission in accordance with the Ministry of Education's
Regulations for International Students Undertaking Studies in Taiwan.
College/Department Scholarship
Please kindly consult your college/department's office for further information.
NTU Loyalty Award
Requirements:
Graduating international or overseas Chinese (including Hong Kong and Macao) degree students of NTU that have been admitted into a graduate program before graduation (Students admitted into master programs should have been in the top half of their class the semester before their application)
Award:
Master Programs:
NTD 100,000
PhD Programs:
NTD 200,000
Further Information:
https://admissions.ntu.edu.tw/loyalty-award/
NTU-Mr. Jamie Lin: South East Asia Scholarship for 2020/2021 Spring Semester (
PDF
)
Application Eligibility: (1) Indonesia, Philippines, or Vietnam nationality. (2) Current bachelor or master student in NTU (3) Last semester's GPA is 2.7 or above
※ If you're a master student and you didn't have any credits last semester because you were working on the thesis, you can also apply for the scholarship.
Coverage and Amount: TWD 60,000/Semester
Application Documents:
Application form
*1 ( with the enrollment stamp of 110-1 semester)
Transcript for 109-2 semester (You can get it at Office of Academic Affairs)
109-2 semester Reward Record*1 (You can download it from myNTU)
The purpose of studying in NTU( Including your family status, the past learning experience, the reason you chose to study in Taiwan, and the future expectations and plans. You can write in either English or Chinese.)
Two recommendation letters from professors or seniors.
How to Apply:
Please submit the application documents to OIA
by email
at
intstudent@ntu.edu.tw
(hard copies will not be accepted).
Please combine all the documents in
one PDF
according to the
sequence of "
Application Documents
".
Kindly name the
PDF file and your email subject
as "
Lin Zhichen Southeast Asia Scholarship Application_Name_Student ID
".
Your application will not be processed if you do not follow the steps above.
Application Deadline:
Before November 5 (Friday),
2021
※ Applicants cannot obtain another scholarship at the same time.
OIA has sent out email notification to the scholarship recipients, if you do not receive the email from OIA, your application to the scholarship was not approved by the institution.

Financial Assistance Grant for International Students (
PDF
)
Available Degree
:
current international degree students
Coverage and Amount:
Tuition waiver + Monthly stipend TWD 6,000/month
Duration:
1 year (2021.09.01-2022.08.31)
How to Apply:
Every college holds the authority to the application procedures. Please kindly consult the
college offices' coordinator
for further application information.
Application Period:
until May 7th, 2021
Please note that
Financial Assistance Grant for International Students
is exclusive. If you will be having another scholarship provided by the government or the university during 2021.09.01-2022.08.31, you cannot apply for this scholarship.
OIA has sent out email notification to the scholarship recipients, if you do not receive the email from OIA, your application to the scholarship was not approved by your college.
