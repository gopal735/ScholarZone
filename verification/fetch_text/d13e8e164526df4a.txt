University of Oulu tuition fees and scholarships for international applicants
Explore tuition fees, Early Bird discounts and tuition fee waiver scholarships available for international applicants to the University of Oulu&#039;s Bachelor&#039;s and Master&#039;s degree programmes. The information on this page applies to students admitted during the autumn 2026 or spring 2027 admission rounds for studies beginning in the 2027-2028 academic year.
Tuition fee liability
Tuition fees apply to students admitted to English-taught Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes who are not citizens of an EU/EEA countries or Switzerland and who do not hold a residence permit that exempts them from tuition fees.
The tuition fee covers the costs of tuition but does not include living expenses such as accommodation, meals, transportation or other personal costs.
Am I required to pay the tuition fees?
Tuition fee is charged from the non-EU/EEA students studying in the English degree programmes and who do not hold a Finnish residence permit that gives the exemption from tuition fees.
The exemption from the tuition fee is given in the following cases:
Citizens of EU/EEA countries or Switzerland or the family members of such persons who have a residence card for a family member of an EU citizen in Finland
permanent (type P) residence permit in Finland or the family members of such persons who have a residence card for a family member of residence permit P holder in Finland
a long-term resident’s EU residence permit for third-country citizens (type P-EU permit) in Finland
a residence permit based on temporary protection
an EU Blue Card in Finland
Right of residence under the withdrawal agreement (Brexit residence permit)
continuous (type A) residence permit in Finland
IF
it is granted on the basis of employment
AND
if the applicant’s first residence permit in Finland was not granted on the basis of studies
continuous (type A) residence permit in Finland based on family ties
IF
the first residence permit of the family member was not granted on the basis of studies
More information is available at the website of
Finnish National Agency of Education

Payment and refund information
Where, how and when should I pay my tuition fee?
Tuition fees are paid through the
Flywire
payment platform. You will receive a link to the payment platform in the admission offer email sent by Admissions Services. If your degree programme offers an Early Bird Discount, the discount is granted if the tuition fee is paid within three weeks of the admission results being published. You will also receive a reminder including the Early Bird payment deadline and a link to the payment portal. You may also request a payment link from Admissions Services by email. Please follow the payment instructions provided in the email and in the payment portal.
Tuition fees, Early Bird Discounts and Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarships vary by degree programme. Please refer to the tables above for programme-specific information.
New students must pay the full first-year tuition fee in a single payment by the deadline. The fee cannot be paid in instalments.
Read more about the
Bachelor's application timeline and deadlines
and
Master's admission deadlines.
Can I get a refund of my tuition fee?
Tuition fees may be refunded in the following circumstances:
Student has been accepted conditionally, and conditions are not fulfilled (study right is cancelled).
Student relinquishes their right to study during the registration period.
Student receives a negative residence permit decision and cannot enter the country, and therefore cannot carry out their studies nor
has not gained any study credits during the academic year
. Remotely started studies and even minor credits prevent the start of refunding process.
A student’s residence permit status changes before the end of the registration period such that they are no longer liable for payment and the student has presented their new residence permit card before the end of the registration period.
Education, to which a student has been accepted, will not be arranged or in so-called force majeure–situations.
In the case of a delayed decision by the Finnish Immigration Service (obligation to demonstrate).
If you register as absent during your first academic year for one of the reasons specified in Section 39 of the Universities Act (558/2009):
Service under the Conscription Act (1438/2007), Non-Military Service Act (1446/2007), or Act on Women's Voluntary Military Service (194/1995).
Parental leave related to the birth or adoption of a child (53/2022).
Inability to begin studies due to a personal illness or injury (325/2015).
N.B. Should there be any study credits during the academic year, online or present, tuition fee is not refunded.
Refunding of tuition fees will be carried out in full with the exception of any possible banking or service fees. Tuition fees are returned to the payer (e.g. sponsors). Otherwise, confirmation that the return can be made to another receiver is required. Tuition fee payment and refund issues belong to responsibilities of the faculties.
For more detailed information about refunding process kindly send an email to your faculty´s study services.

Frequently asked questions
Tuition fees and scholarships
How much is the tuition fee?
Tuition fees vary by degree programme. Please see the tables above for current tuition fees, Early Bird Discounts and Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarships.
Do you offer scholarships?
Yes. Scholarship opportunities vary by degree programme. Selected degree programmes offer an Early Bird Discount for first-year students, and some degree programmes also offer Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarships for continuing students from the second year onwards. Please see the programme-specific tables above for details.
Are scholarships available for first-year students?
No scholarships are available for first-year students. However, selected degree programmes offer an Early Bird Discount for students who pay their tuition fee within three weeks of the admission results being published.
What is a Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarship?
The availability of Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarships varies by degree programme. Where available, the scholarship reduces the annual tuition fee by a programme-specific percentage and is awarded to continuing students who meet the eligibility requirements.
How can I receive a Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarship?
Please note that Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarships are not available in all degree programmes. To see whether a scholarship is available for your degree programme, please refer to the programme-specific tables above. To be eligible, students must complete at least 60 ECTS credits during the preceding academic year in accordance with their approved study plan, including the required Finnish language studies. Additional programme-specific requirements may apply.
Do you offer full scholarships covering 100% of the tuition fee?
No. The University of Oulu does not offer scholarships that cover 100% of the tuition fee. Depending on the degree programme, students may be eligible for an Early Bird Discount and/or a Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarship. Please check the programme-specific tables above for information on the discounts and scholarships available for your degree programme.
When planning your studies, please ensure that you have sufficient funding for tuition fees and living expenses. Scholarships and fee waivers are intended to reduce costs but do not cover all study-related expenses
Can I pay my tuition fee in instalments?
No. New students must pay the full first-year tuition fee in a single payment by the deadline.
