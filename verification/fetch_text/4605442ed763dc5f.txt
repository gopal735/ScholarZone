Bursaries
Your future potential is important to us, and we are committed to encouraging applicants who will bring valuable contributions to the overall class. If you need financial assistance, please be sure to apply for a Bursary at the same time as your admissions application and contact one of our loan providers (see our
External Funding
section for more information).
Deadline to apply: same as admissions application
Your bursary application form can be found directly on your admissions application
Criteria: Proven financial need, good academic results, strong reference letter, and steady career progression
Recipients confirmed on an ongoing basis
External funding
Applications for external funding should ideally be submitted as soon as you are accepted into the program if you want to ensure timely disbursement for the third and fourth tuition installments. If you want to forecast your budget in advance, you might also be able to apply before being accepted.
Should you be unable to secure a loan based on nationality restrictions, please contact our Finance Committee, and we will work with you to investigate alternative solutions.
