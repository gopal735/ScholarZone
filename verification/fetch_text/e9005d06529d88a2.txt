International Bachelor’s and Master’s Programmes at the University of Oulu
Interested in studying in Finland? Looking for a bachelor&#039;s or master´s degree taught in English?
The University of Oulu provides a strong academic foundation and an affordable living environment for students. Housing opportunities are highly competitive compared to other cities globally. Our international students report a high level of satisfaction with the teaching they receive.
Welcome to Oulu – the European Capital of Culture 2026 – a city of urban creativity and northern nature! Oulu is located just a couple of hours&#039; drive from the Arctic circle and Finnish Lapland.
High value learning experience
The University of Oulu offers over 22 Master’s degree programmes (2 years) and four Bachelor´s and Master´s degree programmes (3 + 2 years) taught in English. Applying to these takes place annually in the first joint application to higher education in Finland.
In addition, we offer a joint European Master's programme which has specific admissions.
International students at the University of Oulu are particularly pleased with the quality of accommodation and overall value for money (International Student Barometer 2025).

Education
Finnish education expertise is known all over the world. Education and training through the entire human life cycle is in the core of educational sciences that provide qualifications for many kinds of educational expert tasks in a diversified world.

Oulu School of Architecture
University of Oulu has the northernmost architecture school in the world. The Oulu School of Architecture educates architects to the profession, providing its graduates with high level qualifications for demanding and versatile occupations of building design and urban planning.
Master's programme
Architecture (2 years)
Health Sciences
University of Oulu educates future health sciences experts that can analyze large data of global scale health phenomena.

Oulu Mining School
Oulu Mining School's education covers the entire mining value chain. Graduates know the technical, geological, financial, regulatory, environmental and social aspects of sustainable mining. Learning takes place in a unique mineral processing mini factory.

University of Oulu in short
Founded in 1958, the University of Oulu is one of the largest universities in Finland and one of the northernmost universities in the world. University has 8 faculties, 14 800 students, and 4 300 employees in two campuses, Linnanmaa and Kontinkangas.
We are ranked within the top three
percent of all universities in the world.
