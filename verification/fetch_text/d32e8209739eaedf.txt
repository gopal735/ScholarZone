Dr. S. Radhakrishnan UGC Post-Doctoral Fellowship
Back
Name of the Scheme:
Dr. S. Radhakrishnan UGC Post-Doctoral Fellowship
Objective of Scheme:
The objective of these fellowships is to provide an opportunity to carry out the advanced studies and research in Sciences, Engineering and Technology, Humanities & Social Sciences including languages in Indian Universities/Institutions.
Eligibility:
Only the unemployed candidates who have been awarded the Ph.D. degree are eligible to apply.
Slots:
900
Tenure:
3 years
Financial Assistance:
Fellowship:
Rs. 50,000/- per month
Contingency:
Rs.50,000/- per annum
Escorts/Reader Assistance:
Rs.3000/-p.m.(fixed) in cases of PWD (Person with disability)
Remark:
Only Old cases are being settled
