Practicum project focuses on helping support displaced entrepreneurs
A team of CMU-Africa students spent last semester on a practicum helping Inkomoko shift their client services from a manual system to a data-driven approach to provide an early warning for refugee-led microenterprises. Inkomoko provides affordable loans and support to forcibly displaced entrepreneurs and host communities on the margin of financial systems.

Practicum project: connecting patients to hospitals
