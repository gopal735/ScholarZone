Student Scheme
Junior Research Fellowship in Science, Humanities and Social Sciences
Back
Name of the Scheme:
Junior Research Fellowship in Science, Humanities and Social Sciences
Objective of Scheme:
To provide Financial assistance to NET/JRF-qualified candidates to undertake advanced studies and research leading to M. Phil. / Ph. D. Degree in Humanities and Social Sciences including Languages and Sciences.
Eligibility:
Candidates who have qualified in NET-JRF or the UGC-CSIR joint test. However, the registration for the M.Phil/PhD is made by the Universities/Institutions/Colleges within stipulated period of two years from the date of declaration of result or date of issue of award letter/date of joining the fellowship.
Slots:
11750
Tenure:
5 years or submission of thesis whichever is earlier
Financial Assistance:
Fellowship:
JRF
@Rs. 37,000/-pm for 2 years
SRF
@Rs. 42,000/- pm for remaining period
Contingency A (for Humanities & Social Sciences)
Rs. 10,000/- pa for JRF
Rs. 20,500/- pa for SRF
Contingency B (for Science scholars)
Rs. 12,000/- pa for JRF
Rs. 25,000/- pa for SRF
