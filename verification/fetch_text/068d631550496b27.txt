GKS Scholarsip
Government-invited international scholarship students
Government: Tuition and living expenses, research expenses, insurance premiums
University: Admission fee, amount exceeding 5 million won
Master’s:
4 semesters
PhD:
6 semesters
Yonsei International Companion Scholarship
Eligibility: Full-time international students enrolled in regular semesters through the international student admission track at the Graduate School
Ineligible
1. Students receiving full tuition scholarships(from Graduate School) for regular semesters
2. Students in the College of Medicine, College of Dentistry, or College of Nursing
3. Students in contract-based programs
4. Graduate School students at Mirae Campus
Master's Program:
KRW 2,000,000 per semester
Doctoral Program:
KRW 3,000,000 per semester
Integrated Program:
KRW 2,000,000 per semester
Regular semester
(4 semesters for master’s and PhD,
6 semesters for integrated master’s and PhD program)
