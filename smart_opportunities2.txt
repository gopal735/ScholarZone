All SMART scholars fall into one of the two types of scholars.\n\nRecruitment Scholars\nMost SMART Scholarships are recruitment awards. Recruitment scholars are those who are not employed in a permanent DoW civilian position by a SMART Sponsoring Facility at the time of award.\n\nRetention Scholars\nRetention scholars are individuals who receive a SMART Scholarship and are employed in a full-time, permanent or renewable term, DoW civilian position by a SMART Sponsoring Facility at the time of award. Retention scholars will remain employed by their Sponsoring Facility while pursuing their SMART-funded degree and fulfill their service commitment there.\n\nAs a scholarship-for-service program, recipients receive full-tuition coverage in exchange for a commitment to work as a DoW civilian. Scholars commit to one year of DoW civilian employment for every year of SMART-sponsored degree funding. SMART takes the financial burden off STEM students, so they can focus on what matters. As such, SMART scholars must maintain a 3.0 GPA.
