## 1. MAECI Italian Government Scholarships

### 1.1 MAECI Scholarships for Foreign Students and IRE (A.Y. 2026/2027)
- **Official Name:** Scholarships for foreign students and Italian citizens living abroad awarded by the Italian Government
- **Provider:** MAECI (Ministero degli Affari Esteri e della Cooperazione Internazionale)
- **Official Source:** https://www.esteri.it/en/servizi-opportunita/opportunita/borse-di-studio/per-cittadini-stranieri/borsestudio_stranieri/
- **Application Portal:** https://studyinitaly.esteri.it
- **Deadline:** 26 March 2026, 14:00 CET (CLOSED for 2026/27)
- **Eligible Countries:** Published list at https://studyinitaly.esteri.it/ListaBandi
- **Degree Levels:** Master's (Laurea Magistrale), AFAM (1st/2nd cycle), PhD, Research projects, Italian language courses (3 months)
- **Funding:** €10,800 total (9 months), paid in 3 instalments (€5,500 + €2,300 + €3,000)
- **Benefits:** Monthly allowance, tuition fee exemption (regional tax €140 + stamp duty €16 still due)
- **Fully Funded:** No (partial - living allowance only, no accommodation)
- **Verification Status:** Official MAECI call published, deadline passed for 2026/27
- **2027/28 Status:** No call published yet; historical pattern suggests March 2027 deadline

### 1.2 Invest Your Talent in Italy (IYT) — 11th Edition A.Y. 2026/2027
- **Official Name:** Invest Your Talent in Italy scholarship programme
- **Provider:** MAECI / ICE / Uni-Italia
- **Official Source:** https://investyourtalentapplication.esteri.it/SitoIYT/EN/pdf/CALL_IYT_ENGLISH_2026.pdf
- **Application Portal:** https://investyourtalentapplication.esteri.it
- **Deadline:** 11 May 2026, 18:00 CET (CLOSED for 2026/27)
- **Eligible Countries:** Argentina, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bangladesh, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Egypt, Ethiopia, Ghana, India, Indonesia, Iran, Kazakhstan, Mexico, Mongolia, Paraguay, Peru, PRC, Tunisia, Turkey, Uruguay, Vietnam
- **Degree Levels:** Master's / Postgraduate (English-taught)
- **Funding:** €10,800 (9 months), paid quarterly (€4,800 + €3,000 + €3,000)
- **Benefits:** Tuition fee waiver (except regional tax €140 + stamp duty €16), mandatory internship, Italian language course
- **Fully Funded:** No (partial - stipend + tuition waiver, no accommodation)
- **Verification Status:** Official call published, deadline passed
- **2027/28 Status:** 12th edition expected April-May 2027

## 8. Key Data Fields for Each Scholarship Record

**Application:** application_open_date, application_deadline, deadline_timezone, application_url, official_source_url, required_documents, selection_process, renewal_conditions, compatibility_rules
