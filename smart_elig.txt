SMART provides merit-based scholarships to bachelor's, master's, and doctoral students pursuing STEM degrees. Eligibility was expanded to include incoming college freshmen with no prior college credit, extending the opportunity to high school seniors.\n\nSMART Scholarship recipients receive full tuition, annual stipends, mentorship, health and book allowances, and conduct summer internships at a DoW facility before embarking on a DoW civilian career after graduation. The first step to a rewarding STEM career is reviewing the eligibility requirements, starting with the SMART STEM disciplines. These disciplines are general categories – specific majors or fields of study may fall under one or more disciplines on this list. All scholarship applicants should be pursuing, or intend to pursue, a degree in one of these disciplines. To explore a list of common majors that align with SMART STEM disciplines, click the 'Check Qualifying Majors' button below.\n\nAerospace and Aeronautical Engineering\nBiomedical Engineering\nBiosciences\nChemical Engineering\nChemistry\nCivil Engineering\nCognitive, Neural, and Behavioral Sciences\nComputer Science and Engineering\nData Science and Analytics\nElectrical Engineering\nGeosciences\nIndustrial and Systems Engineering\nInformation Sciences\nMaterials Science and Engineering\nMathematics\nMechanical Engineering\nNaval Architecture and Ocean Engineering\nNuclear Engineering\nOceanography\nOperations Research\nPhysics\nSoftware Engineering\n\nPlease note, non-technical degrees, including management, arts, or humanities, are not approved or funded by SMART.\n\nCHECK QUALIFYING MAJORS SMART OPPORTUNITIES APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS CONTACT US APPLY NOW\nScholarship Eligibility for 2026\nBe a citizen of the United States, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, or United Kingdom\nBe 18 years of age or older by January 1, 2028\nHave and maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale\nBe currently pursuing, or intend to pursue, education in Fall 2027*\nPlanning to attend, or enrolled in, a regionally accredited U.S. college or university\nAble to obtain and maintain a security clearance**\nBe requesting between 1-5 years of degree funding (funding begins August 2027)\nComplete summer internships for awards greater than 1.5 years\nAccept a one-for-one post graduation service commitment as a civilian employee of the Department of War\n*High school seniors and incoming college freshmen who will be pursuing a bachelor’s degree in fall 2027 are eligible to apply.\n**Security clearances are initiated by a scholar's Sponsoring Facility after award.\nFrequently Asked Eligibility Questions\n\nUnderstanding eligibility criteria is a crucial first step in your SMART application. Awardees commit to one year of DoW civilian employment at their Sponsoring Facility for every year of SMART-sponsored degree funding. For this reason, reviewing Sponsoring Facility information is paramount. It ensures alignment with the Sponsoring Facility's objectives and can help determine if your major falls under one of the SMART STEM disciplines.\n\nSPONSORING FACILITY\nHear What SMART Scholars Are Saying\n\nJuliet Swinea, Ph.D.\n\nPhD, Civil Engineering\n\n\"I have been put in the right rooms at the right times, and have been encouraged to present, take initiative, and engage with industry, academic, and government partners on behalf of the Department.\"\n\nZaire Wade\n\nMS, Computer Science and Engineering\n\n“SMART has broadened my global perspective of understanding that everything is interconnected, and I believe that the Department really speaks to that purpose and that mission, and understanding that your education can help individuals all over the world.
