## Cycle vs Programme Classification
| Concept | Definition | Example |
|---------|------------|---------|
| **Programme** | Ongoing scholarship initiative | "DAAD Study Scholarships" |
| **Cycle** | Specific application round with deadline | "DAAD Study Scholarships 2026 (deadline 2026-08-31)" |

**Each target record = One cycle of a programme**
- Deleting the cycle record ≠ Deleting the programme
- Programme metadata persists in other cycle records
- New cycle records will be added when 2027 cycles officially announced
