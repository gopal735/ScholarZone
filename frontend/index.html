Scholarship basics, in plain words.

What does “funded” actually cover?

Typical patterns only. Every programme sets its own rules.

When to start

Counting back from the start of your degree.

  1. 18 to 12 monthsResearch programmes, check eligibility and shortlist 3 to 5.
  2. 12 to 9 monthsBook your language test and start your statement of purpose.
  3. 9 to 6 monthsAsk referees early. Collect transcripts and certificates.
  4. 6 to 3 monthsFinish applications and submit a few days before each deadline.
  5. 3 to 0 monthsPrepare for interviews, your visa and travel.

A typical plan. Deadlines differ, so always check each programme.

Mistakes that cost applicants

✗

One generic statement for every programme

Instead: tailor your opening and the “why this programme” paragraph each time.

✗

Starting once the deadline is close

Instead: referees and test scores take weeks, so begin months ahead.

✗

Skipping the eligibility page

Instead: check nationality, degree and language rules before writing a word.

✗

Uploading unclear documents

Instead: use clean scans in the exact file format the portal asks for.
