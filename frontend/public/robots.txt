# ScholarZone - public crawl policy
#
# The public catalogue is meant to be indexed: the directory, the country
# pages and every scholarship detail page are the product.
#
# The private surface is not. Admin, Saved and Compare either require a session
# or render per-user state, and a search engine reaching them sees an empty or
# broken shell. Compare and Saved are additionally near-duplicates of the
# directory, so indexing them would dilute the pages that matter.

User-agent: *
Allow: /ScholarZone/
Allow: /ScholarZone/scholarships

Disallow: /ScholarZone/admin
Disallow: /ScholarZone/login
Disallow: /ScholarZone/register
Disallow: /ScholarZone/saved
Disallow: /ScholarZone/compare

# Crawling the single-page app shell is pointless and can be expensive.
Disallow: /*?*

Sitemap: https://gopal735.github.io/ScholarZone/sitemap.xml
