## Phase 4: Inclusion Criteria (UNCHANGED)

For each candidate scholarship, verify at least ONE:
- [ ] Official application window is CURRENTLY OPEN
- [ ] Official website confirms a FUTURE opening date
- [ ] Official programme announcement confirms a FUTURE cycle and intake

**MUST HAVE:**
- [ ] Authoritative official source URL (government, university, official programme site)
- [ ] No fabrication of dates, amounts, eligibility, URLs
- [ ] Distinction from fee discounts, internships, jobs, unrelated grants
