You’re in the right place! Here’s everything you need to know about applying for the Science, Mathematics, and Research for Transformation (SMART) Scholarship-for-Service Program.\n\nTo start, explore the Eligibility page to learn about the program’s requirements, including eligible STEM disciplines, and visit the SMART Videos page for helpful tips and application overviews. Applications for the SMART Program are accepted annually, from August to December. Be sure to submit your online application by 5:00 p.m. EST on the first Friday in December, as late applications or reference submissions will not be accepted. Supporting materials, such as reference forms, must also be submitted by the deadline. Applicants are typically notified of award decisions in the spring. Please note that application materials will not be returned to applicants.\n\n \n\nWhat You Need to Know Before Applying:\n\nTo begin the application process, complete the registration page to confirm your eligibility and receive access to the Application Portal. After registering, you’ll receive an email with instructions for setting up your account, logging in, and starting your application. Before accessing the portal, be sure to review the Account Authentication Set Up instructions carefully.\n\nEvery part of the application must be completed for the Submit Application button to appear. You can track your progress using the progress bar across the top of the page, the checklist on the left-hand side, or the subsection trackers within each section. The application is designed to guide you through the process step by step and helps you monitor your status.\n\n \n\nWhat the SMART Application Includes:\n\nPrivacy Act Statement\nApplication Instructions\nProgram Materials\nUpload Resume\nUpload Transcript\nAbout Me\nReferences\nMy Experiences\nProposed Award Details\nPersonal Statement\n\n \n\nAdditional Resources: \n\nFor more detailed information on specific aspects of the application process, refer to these key resources:\n\nTranscripts\nDoW Employee Supervisor Letter\nHow to Submit your SMART Application\n\n \n\nPro Tips:\n\nStay updated on your application status to ensure all required reference forms are received by the SMART Program before the deadline.\nThe “Submit Application” button must be clicked before the deadline for your application to be considered.\nYou can save an in-progress application and return later to update it. If you’ve already submitted your application and need to make changes, you can reopen it—but make sure to re-submit it before the deadline!\n\n \n\n \n\nSMART\nScholarship-for-Service Program\nPhone: (571) 633-7940\nEmail: smart@smartscholarship.org\nOffice Hours:\nMonday - Friday, 9:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time\nOur office is closed on all U.S. federal holidays.\nUseful Links\nHome\nAbout Us\nEligibility\nApplication Instructions\nVideos\nGet Help\nTechnical Help\nFAQ\nContact Us\nStay up to date on SMART announcements.\nFollow us on social media!\n \n \n \n \n "
